(14) Core posterior for one model configuration H (the master equation).
p\left(x_{0:K},\, a,\, \theta_{\mathrm{Core}} \mid z_{\mathrm{Core}},\, H\right) \propto p\left(x_{0}\right) P\left(a \mid H\right) p\left(\theta_{\mathrm{Core}} \mid H\right) \prod_{k=1}^{K} p\left(x_{k} \mid x_{k-1},\, a,\, \theta_{\mathrm{Core}},\, H\right)\, L\left(z_{\mathrm{Core}} \mid x_{0:K}\right)
  where:
    x_{k}  —  Aircraft state at time t_k: latitude, longitude, altitude, Mach, control angle, wind error and, with the fuel model, the fuel in each tank [—]; Particle state (crates/flight)
    x_{0:K}  —  The whole path, from t_0 to t_K [—]; One particle
    t_{0},\ t_{K}  —  Start and end times of the core estimate [UTC]; 18:01:49 and 00:19:37 UTC
    k  —  Index of the observation times (satellite and radar) [—]; Propagation between them in 10 s steps (5 s in manoeuvres)
    a  —  Autopilot mode: true heading, magnetic heading, true track, magnetic track or lateral navigation (LNAV) [—]; One filter per mode; LNAV can revert to heading hold
    \theta_{\mathrm{Core}}  —  Fixed values for each path: manoeuvre time constant τ; with the fuel model also κ, κ_x, r, δ and the one-engine drift-down values [—]; Drawn once per path; τ refreshed after each resampling
    H  —  Model configuration or conditional hypothesis (e.g. Davey model; Davey model + fuel + radar; waypoint routes) [—]; Table of configurations
    z_{\mathrm{Core}}  —  Evidence used by the core: BTO, BFO and radar [—]; Satellite log and radar fixes
    p(x_{0})  —  Prior at 18:01:49 UTC: position σ 0.5 NM, track σ 1° [—]; Davey et al. (2016), ch. 4
    P(a \mid H)  —  Prior probability of each autopilot mode [—]; Equal, 1/5, in every configuration
    p(x_{k} \mid x_{k-1},\, \ldots)  —  Dynamics: how the state moves from one time to the next [—]; Davey ch. 6–8 (Ornstein–Uhlenbeck cruise, manoeuvres), plus fuel burn when used
    L(z_{\mathrm{Core}} \mid x_{0:K})  —  Likelihood of all core evidence along the path [—]; Equation (15)
    \propto  —  Is proportional to; the right side is normalised to integrate to one [—]; —
  One-sentence replacement: The posterior of each path is its prior, times its dynamics, times the likelihood of the evidence along it, normalised over all paths.

(15) Core likelihood: the factors that each configuration switches on or off.
L\left(z_{\mathrm{Core}} \mid x_{0:K}\right) = L_{\mathrm{BTO}}\, L_{\mathrm{BFO}}\, L_{\mathrm{Radar}}\, I_{\mathrm{Power}}
  where:
    L_{\mathrm{BTO}}  —  Likelihood of the burst timing offsets [—]; Equation (16); all configurations
    L_{\mathrm{BFO}}  —  Likelihood of the burst frequency offsets, with the unknown bias integrated out [—]; Equation (17); all configurations
    L_{\mathrm{Radar}}  —  Likelihood of the radar plots after 18:01:49 UTC [—]; Equation (18); set to 1 in the Davey reproduction
    I_{\mathrm{Power}}  —  Engine power at 00:10:59 UTC: 1 if at least one engine runs, else 0 [—]; Equation (19); set to 1 in the Davey reproduction
  One-sentence replacement: The core likelihood is the product of the BTO, BFO and radar likelihoods and the requirement for engine power at 00:10:59 UTC.

(16) BTO likelihood.
L_{\mathrm{BTO}} = \prod_{j=1}^{10} \mathcal{N}\left(z_{\mathrm{BTO},j};\ F_{\mathrm{BTO}}\left(x_{j}\right),\ \sigma_{\mathrm{BTO},j}^{2}\right)
  where:
    z_{\mathrm{BTO},j}  —  Measured burst timing offset j: 18:25:34, 18:28:05, 18:28:14, 19:41, 20:41, 21:41, 22:41, 00:10:59, 00:19:29 and 00:19:37 UTC [μs]; 18:25:34 and 00:19:37 values: R1200 BTO, empirical offset applied (−5 and −4 × 7,820 μs)
    F_{\mathrm{BTO}}(x)  —  Functional model: round-trip delay ground station–satellite–aircraft, minus the fixed offset [μs]; Davey ch. 5; fixed offset 495,679 μs
    \sigma_{\mathrm{BTO},j}  —  Standard deviation of BTO j [μs]; 29 (R1200), 43 (R1200 log-on acknowledge), 63 (R600 log-on request)
    \mathcal{N}(z;\ \mu,\ \sigma^{2})  —  Normal density of z with mean μ and variance σ² [—]; —
  One-sentence replacement: Each of the ten BTOs is normally distributed about the value the path predicts, with standard deviations of 29 to 63 μs.

(17) BFO likelihood with the bias integrated out, written as a product of one-step prediction errors.
L_{\mathrm{BFO}} = \int \prod_{i=1}^{9} \mathcal{N}\left(z_{\mathrm{BFO},i};\ F_{\mathrm{BFO}}\left(x_{i}\right) + b,\ \sigma_{\mathrm{BFO}}^{2}\right) \mathcal{N}\left(b;\ \bar{b}_{0},\ P_{0}\right) \mathrm{d}b = \prod_{i=1}^{9} \mathcal{N}\left(\nu_{i};\ 0,\ s_{i}^{2}\right)
  where:
    z_{\mathrm{BFO},i}  —  Measured burst frequency offset i: 18:28:05, 18:28:14, 18:39:55*, 19:41, 20:41, 21:41, 22:41, 23:15:02*, 00:10:59 UTC (*mean of the C-channel values) [Hz]; No 00:19 BFO is used in the core
    F_{\mathrm{BFO}}(x)  —  Functional model: uplink and downlink Doppler, aircraft compensation, satellite and EAFC terms [Hz]; Davey ch. 5; vertical rate included except in the Davey reproduction
    b  —  Unknown constant BFO bias of the aircraft terminal [Hz]; Integrated out exactly, for each path
    \bar{b}_{0},\ P_{0}  —  Prior mean and variance of b [Hz, Hz²]; 150 Hz and (25 Hz)²
    \sigma_{\mathrm{BFO}}  —  BFO measurement standard deviation [Hz]; 7 Hz in the Davey reproduction; 4.32 Hz (Davey Table 5.1) otherwise
    \nu_{i}  —  Prediction error: z_BFO,i − F_BFO(x_i) − b̂_{i−1}, with b̂ the scalar Kalman estimate of b [Hz]; Davey §8.1
    s_{i}^{2}  —  Variance of ν_i: σ²_BFO + P_{i−1} [Hz²]; The same for every path
  One-sentence replacement: The BFO bias is integrated out with a scalar Kalman filter, so each BFO enters through its prediction error.

(18) Radar likelihood after 18:01:49 UTC.
L_{\mathrm{Radar}} = \prod_{r=1}^{5} \left[ (1-\epsilon)\, \mathcal{N}_{2}\left(d_{r};\ \sigma_{r}\right) + \epsilon\, \mathcal{N}_{2}\left(d_{r};\ \sigma_{\mathrm{Outlier}}\right) \right] \prod_{c=1}^{4} \mathcal{N}\left(e_{c};\ 0,\ \sigma_{c}^{2}\right)
  where:
    d_{r}  —  Great-circle distance from the path to radar fix r (18:04:14, 18:05:15, 18:07:16, 18:13:36, 18:22:12 UTC) [NM]; Plot at 18:07:06 excluded
    \mathcal{N}_{2}(d;\ \sigma)  —  Isotropic two-dimensional normal density at distance d: (2πσ²)⁻¹ exp(−d²/2σ²) [NM⁻²]; —
    \sigma_{r}  —  Standard deviation of fix r [NM]; 3 NM at 18:07:16; 5 NM otherwise
    \epsilon,\ \sigma_{\mathrm{Outlier}}  —  Share and spread of the outlier component [—, NM]; 0.1 and 50 NM
    e_{c}  —  Across-track distance from the VAMPI–MEKAR line at 18:15, 18:17, 18:19, 18:21 UTC [NM]; Dense radar band
    \sigma_{c}  —  Standard deviation across track [NM]; 1.5 NM
  One-sentence replacement: The five radar fixes are scored as two-dimensional normal densities with a 10% outlier component, and four times on the VAMPI–MEKAR line by their across-track distance.

(19) Engine power at 00:10:59 UTC.
I_{\mathrm{Power}} = \begin{cases} 1, & m_{\mathrm{Left}}\left(t_{0011}\right) > 0 \ \text{or}\ m_{\mathrm{Right}}\left(t_{0011}\right) > 0 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
  where:
    m_{\mathrm{Left}},\ m_{\mathrm{Right}}  —  Fuel mass in the left and right tanks [kg]; Equation (20)
    t_{0011}  —  Time of the 00:10:59 UTC transmission [UTC]; 00:10:59
    I_{\mathrm{Power}}  —  Indicator: a path with both tanks empty before 00:10:59 gets weight zero [—]; No prior on the time of fuel exhaustion is used
  One-sentence replacement: A path is given zero weight if both tanks are empty before 00:10:59 UTC; results are therefore conditional on engine power at 00:11.

(20) Fuel burn by tank (both engines running; one engine after the first tank is empty).
\dot{m}_{\mathrm{Left}} = -\frac{\kappa\, \kappa_{x}^{\delta_{x}}\, f_{T}\, Q_{\mathrm{Twin}}}{1+r}, \qquad \dot{m}_{\mathrm{Right}} = -\frac{r\, \kappa\, \kappa_{x}^{\delta_{x}}\, f_{T}\, Q_{\mathrm{Twin}}}{1+r}, \qquad \dot{m}_{\mathrm{Live}} = -\kappa\, f_{T}\, Q_{\mathrm{One}}
  where:
    Q_{\mathrm{Twin}},\ Q_{\mathrm{One}}  —  Fuel flow with two engines, and of the live engine with one engine inoperative, from flight level, gross weight and Mach; levels capped at the weight-dependent ceiling [kg/h]; Boeing-derived performance grid; one-engine grid × 0.5 (unit correction)
    f_{T}  —  Temperature factor 1 + 0.003 ΔT_ISA (1 + 0.2 M²) [—]; ΔT_ISA from ERA5 at the aircraft
    \kappa  —  Fuel-flow factor of the path [—]; N(1.0004, 0.0196²); fuel at 18:01:49 = 43,800 − 7,228κ kg
    \kappa_{x},\ \delta_{x}  —  Extra factor on extrapolated flow; δ_x = 1 on such steps, else 0 [—]; N(1.0, 0.046²), provisional
    r  —  Right-to-left flow ratio [—]; N(1.021, 0.008²)
    \delta  —  Left minus right fuel at 18:01:49 UTC (in θ_Core) [kg]; N(221, 120²)
    \dot{m}_{\mathrm{Live}}  —  Burn of the remaining engine after the first tank is empty; the aircraft drifts down to its one-engine ceiling [kg/h]; Drift-down profile provisional
  One-sentence replacement: Fuel is burned from two tanks at the performance-grid flow, scaled by a per-path factor and a temperature term; after the first tank empties, the other engine burns at the one-engine flow while the aircraft drifts down.

(21) Combination over autopilot modes within one configuration H.
p\left(x_{K} \mid z_{\mathrm{Core}},\, H\right) = \sum_{a} P\left(a \mid z_{\mathrm{Core}},\, H\right) p\left(x_{K} \mid z_{\mathrm{Core}},\, a,\, H\right), \qquad P\left(a \mid z_{\mathrm{Core}},\, H\right) \propto P\left(a \mid H\right) p\left(z_{\mathrm{Core}} \mid a,\, H\right)
  where:
    x_{K}  —  State at 00:19:37 UTC; its latitude gives the 00:19 PDF [—]; Final particle state
    p(z_{\mathrm{Core}} \mid a,\, H)  —  Marginal likelihood of mode a (the Bayesian evidence): the likelihood averaged over the prior and the dynamics [—]; Estimated by the filter as the product of its mean incremental weights
    P(a \mid z_{\mathrm{Core}},\, H)  —  Posterior probability of mode a [—]; Normalised over the five modes
  One-sentence replacement: Each autopilot mode is filtered separately, and the modes are combined in proportion to their prior probability times their marginal likelihood.

(22) Goodness-of-fit statistic for one path, and its smallest value in a latitude band.
\chi^{2}\left(x_{0:K}\right) = \sum_{j=1}^{10} \left(\frac{z_{\mathrm{BTO},j} - F_{\mathrm{BTO}}\left(x_{j}\right)}{\sigma_{\mathrm{BTO},j}}\right)^{2} + \sum_{i=1}^{9} \frac{\nu_{i}^{2}}{s_{i}^{2}}, \qquad \chi_{\min}^{2}(\Phi) = \min_{n:\ \varphi_{K}^{(n)} \in \Phi} \chi^{2}\left(x_{0:K}^{(n)}\right)
  where:
    \chi^{2}  —  Sum of squared, standardised BTO and BFO residuals along one path (19 values) [—]; Radar and fuel not included
    \Phi  —  A 0.5° band of latitude at 00:19:37 UTC [deg]; —
    n,\ \varphi_{K}^{(n)}  —  Path index and its latitude at 00:19:37 UTC [—, deg]; All paths of all filter runs of the configuration
    \chi_{\min}^{2}(\Phi)  —  Best fit found in band Φ [—]; Depends on the sampled paths, not on their weights
  One-sentence replacement: For each path, χ² is the sum of squared standardised BTO and BFO residuals; since s_i² is the same for all paths, differences in χ² equal −2 times differences in log-likelihood.
