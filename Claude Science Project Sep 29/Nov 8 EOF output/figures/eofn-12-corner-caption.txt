Joint distributions of four impact quantities, Davey model with the fuel model: latitude, distance from the 7th arc (positive to the south-east), time from 00:19:29 to impact, and impact kinetic energy. Panels (a), (c), (f) and (j) show each quantity alone, scaled to its peak; the other panels show pairs. Grey shading: regions holding 95 % and 50 % of the probability with the BTO values only. Black lines: the same regions with both BFOs and Holland's transient bias added. Time and energy use logarithmic scales.
