Figure X. Effect of the recovered-debris evidence on the distribution of the impact position along the 7th arc. (a) Probability density of impact latitude from the satellite and flight data alone, and with the drift likelihood of the flaperon alone, of all nine confirmed or almost-certain finds, and of the nine finds together with the absence of finds on Australian coasts at detection ratios 0.25 and 1. (b) Shift of the mean impact latitude against the Australian detection ratio, relative to the coasts where debris was found; grey bars join the two independent particle halves. (c) Shift produced by each find alone and by all finds except that one. Drift likelihood from forward simulation in two ocean reanalyses (equal weight), with 32 ocean-environment realisations per release point south of 33.25 S and 16 north of it; the 00:19 satellite data are not used.
