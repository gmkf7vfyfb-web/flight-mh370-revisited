# Close-ups with the impact PDF without H in the background (Pete, 10 Oct 2026)

Same data throughout: core (b), 00:19 R600 BTO Only, transmitting at 00:19:37, log-on not from fuel exhaustion. The
panel is after Phase 2 + Bluefin-21 + Ocean Infinity 2018 + 2025-26 (the old panel b; panel a is dropped). All labels
on the standard close-ups apply.

The background is the impact PDF **without H**: the flight posterior alone, after the same searches. Only 29 % of it
lies inside this frame; most of it is further south-west along the arc. It is drawn three ways:
- **contours**: its 50 % and 90 % regions as dashed lines;
- **points**: 6,000 points drawn in proportion to its probability;
- **shade**: its density as shading, scaled to its 99th percentile inside the frame.

Files: `closeup-r600-bto+alive-{colour,seabed}-bg-{contours,points,shade}.{png,pdf}`; without a background,
`closeup-r600-bto+alive-{colour,seabed}.{png,pdf}`.

**"Grade C"** is the project's source grade. A: peer-reviewed or official primary source, read with pages. B: official,
cited but not read. C: online or community analysis, used as argument, never as data. For the Ocean Infinity layers it
means a community tracing (MH370-CAPTION) of published map imagery and vessel tracks. Ocean Infinity has released no
survey geometry, so the layer is a planning envelope with a coverage fraction applied, not measured swath coverage
(`engine/hypotheses/seabed-search/coverage/PROVENANCE.md`).
