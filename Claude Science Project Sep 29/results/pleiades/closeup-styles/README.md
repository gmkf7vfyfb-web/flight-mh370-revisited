# Three alternative designs for the close-ups (Pete, 10 Oct 2026)

Same data in all three, from `prepare/closeup_styles.py`: core (b), **00:19 R600 BTO Only**, transmitting at 00:19:37,
log-on not from fuel exhaustion, strata mixed by P(family). Every label on the current close-ups applies.

- **A, colour** (`style-A-colour`). Four small multiples with a blue density scale and two contours (50 % and 90 %).
  The searched areas are translucent fills, so the probability shows through them. The areas and means sit in a table
  under the maps instead of inside the axes.
- **B, seabed** (`style-B-seabed`). One large map of the headline panel (Pléiades + COSMO F1–F4, after every search)
  on GEBCO 2026 shaded relief with 1 km depth contours. The 50/90/99 % regions are warm translucent bands. Beside the
  map, a latitude-PDF strip compares the four variants.
- **C, arc frame** (`style-C-arc-frame`). The PDF re-projected into nautical miles along the 7th arc and north-west
  of it. The search bands become horizontal strips, so how far inside or outside the arc the probability lies, and
  which band covers it, read off directly. A marginal on the right gives the share north-west of the arc.
