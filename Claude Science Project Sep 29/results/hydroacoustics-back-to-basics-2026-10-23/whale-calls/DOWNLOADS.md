SEANOE search for further OHASISBIO / DEFLOHYDRO whale catalogues (fin whale, pygmy blue populations): no other
public catalogue covering March 2014 was found. Later OHASISBIO whale work (Torterotot et al. 2020 DSR II; Leroy et al.
2021 Sci Rep) did not release per-call tables.
