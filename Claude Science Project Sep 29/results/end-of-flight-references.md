# End of flight: citation ledger

The end-of-flight module's ledger under the standing rule of 9 October (`coordination/architecture.md`,
~01:20 UTC, location corrected ~02:25 UTC). The matching BibTeX is `results/end-of-flight-references.bib`.

**Page convention:**
- Printed pages come from the running header or footer, and are given as "p."
- Where the text extraction carries no printed number, the physical page is given as "PDF p." and flagged.
- **"Not yet read in primary form"** means the value reached this module through the brief or another
  module's verified ledger. Its page must be checked before the paper.
- No unauthorised copy of a copyrighted work is cited. Manuals that circulate only as mirrored copies
  (777 FCTM, operator QRHs) are used as calibration targets with a declared sensitivity, and are **not
  cited** (`aero.rs` provenance table).
