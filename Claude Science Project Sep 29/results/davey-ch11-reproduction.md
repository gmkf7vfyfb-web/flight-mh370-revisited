(UTC). Inbox item 4 and ruling A1 in `coordination/OCEAN_DRIFT.md`. Three reasons this is not

1. The posterior it updates, `no-exhaustion-prior`, **fails split-half convergence**: 0.9020

3. Two of the method's steps are **not specified in print**: the join criteria and the size of

## 1. The method as printed

Source: Davey, Gordon, Holland, Rutten and Williams, *Bayesian Methods in the Search for MH370*,
SpringerBriefs, ch. 11 "Ongoing Refinement", printed pp. 101–109. DOI 10.1007/978-981-10-0379-0_11,
resolved through Crossref on 8 Oct 2026 (title, authors and pages 101–109 confirmed). Pages below
are **printed** pages from the running headers. The PDF is in the artifact store,
version e006e598.

## 2. Assumptions made here (every one provisional and a config parameter)

| ID | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A-und | Undrogued = `time > drogue_lost_date`, `drogue_lost_date > 0`. Missing ("still attached") and 0 ("uncertain from beginning") are excluded. | The dataset's own convention. Our drogue dates are AOML's current record, not the 2015–16 version Davey used. |
| A-daily | One fix per drifter per day, at 00 UTC. | Neither the 1° (or 0.25°) kernel nor the ±30-day window resolves sub-daily motion. |
| A-domain | Starts {x_p} and {x̃_p} are the same set: undrogued daily fixes in Feb–Apr of any year inside 75–125°E, 45°S–10°N, the Fig. 11.2 panel extent. That gives 92,637 start-days from 1,214 drifters, 1989–2025. | The figure extent is the only printed domain. With the same set, numerator and denominator differ only by arrival weighting. |
| A-join-d / A-join-doy | Partner fix within **150 km**, within **±30 days of the same day of year**, any year. | **Calibrated, not printed.** On 1,500 random fixes this gives a median of 28 distinct partner drifters (10–90 %: 15–39), matching the printed "around 30". In the runs the median is 25 (10–90 %: 11–37). Tight alternative: 50 km, ±15 d, median 4. |
| A-join-when | A head is followed for at most 135 days (⌈538/4⌉), or until its undrogued record ends (death, a gap, or leaving the 20–140°E, 60°S–10°N data box). It is then joined. A synthetic path has at most 4 real segments, i.e. 3 joins. | Four segments must be able to span the 508 ± 30 day window. |
| A-join-pick | Each join picks one partner drifter uniformly (then that drifter's nearest qualifying fix). When the head's own record continues, continuing on itself is one more option. Monte Carlo: 10 paths per start per seed, 4 seeds, pooled to 40 per start. | Preserves probability per start. Davey's literal branching (×30 per join) would multiply paths through dense regions; that is deliberately not reproduced. Mean joins per path 2.95; no partner at 0.06 % of join points. |
| A-R | R is a disc of radius 200 km about Réunion (21.115°S, 55.536°E). Sensitivities at 100 and 400 km. | Not printed. |
| A-window | Arrival means any daily fix inside R at an elapsed time of 478–538 days. | Fig. 11.2 title. |
| A-kde | Gaussian kernel, isotropic in degrees with no cos-latitude factor. Densities per deg². Binned at 0.05° and Gaussian-filtered, zero outside the box. | Matches "1° in latitude and longitude". The binned KDE agrees with the exact KDE to 0.5 % in fixture F1. |
| A-ε-units | ε is added to densities normalised per deg². | Our peaks are 1.8 × 10⁻³ per deg² for all starts and 4.5 × 10⁻³ for arrivers. By eye, Davey's Fig. 11.2 colour bars run to about 2.5 × 10⁻³ and 5 × 10⁻³, so the scale matches and ε plays the same relative role. |
| A-noend | A path with no qualifying partner ends as a non-arrival. | This is Davey's construction, and it inherits the survivor bias that the brief §9 warns about. |
| A-particles | **Each 0.25° map cell is one weighted particle at the cell centre**, weight = density × 0.0625 (the task's ruling). Cells follow `summary.rs::map_cell`: floor index, lower-left origin. That gives 1,022 cells (512 with positive mass), 100 % of mass inside the start domain. | Contract rule 3 is not met: this scores cell centres, not the shared impact samples. That is acceptable for a reproduction only. |
| A-quantiles | Quantiles come from the 0.25° latitude-row histogram, linear within rows. Intervals are **equal-tailed**. | The datasheet's [−37.85, −37.00] and [−38.35, −35.50] are highest-density intervals from the finer 1-D density. The map-row HPD is [−38.00, −37.00] (50 %) and [−38.25, −35.25] (90 %), stored in the JSON. Medians agree: map −37.227 against the datasheet's −37.225. |

## 5. Reading it

1. **The reproduction gives Davey's direction and magnitude.** The pooled update moves the median
   **+2.7 NM north**, about 0.05° of latitude. The ESS fraction is 0.988 and the TV is 0.03. On
   this posterior that is "shifted very slightly to the North, but the effect is negligible". The
   northward sign is reasonably secure at 1°: all four seeds are ≥ 0 and 97.5 % of the partial
   bootstrap is northward. The size is not secure, because the seed s.d. is 2.4 NM. The mean
   moves more, +10 NM, and consistently, because the update acts on the tails rather than the
   body.
2. **The likelihood surface itself is not flat** (Fig., panel a). The ratio is about 2 at
   25–35°S west of 100°E and falls below ¼ south of about 40°S. This is the same broad pattern as
   Davey's Fig. 11.2, lower panels (likelihood colour range 0.5–3). The posterior's main body,
   −38.5 to −34.5°, 95 % of mass, lies on the gradient but spans only about 4° along the arc.
   Across it the mean relative likelihood moves from 0.93 to 1.07, about ±7 %.
3. **The D3 test: how much of "negligible" is resolving power?** Narrowing the kernel from 1° to
   0.5° to 0.25° does **not** enlarge the median shift: 2.7, 1.6 and 1.2 NM at ε = 10⁻⁴. It does
   enlarge the reshaping: TV 0.032 → 0.035 → 0.061 and ESS 0.988 → 0.983 → 0.967. Almost all of
   the extra reshaping is in the tails: the south of −38.5°S is halved again, and the northern
   mode gains. **But the extra reshaping is not resolved.** At 0.25° (ε = 10⁻⁴) the TV between single
   seeds (mean 0.119) exceeds the TV from prior to update (0.095). At 0.25° with ε = 10⁻⁶ the
   single-seed median shift is +0.9 ± 2.9 NM. At 1° the seed-to-seed TV (0.037) is already comparable to the update (0.041).
   **So with the GDP drifter record the narrower kernel buys noise, not signal.** The
   negligibility is mainly a property of the information content of 30 years of undrogued drifters
   and the 508-day transit. It is not mainly a property of the 1° kernel. This qualifies, rather
   than contradicts, the claim in D3 of `results/davey-ch11-alignment.md`: a kernel of about the
   posterior's width does bound the reshaping, but on this data a narrower one cannot be
   supported. That argues for this module's controlled-release design (D1, D2), whose noise is
   set by the ensemble size, not by the historical drifter count. Per the inbox ruling, this is
   stated as a result about resolving power, not as a claim that Davey's result was wrong.
4. **ε (10⁻⁴ against 10⁻⁶) matters little to the summaries** (median +2.75 against +3.02 NM at 1°)
   but **a lot to the likelihood floor**. Over the posterior's support the minimum denominator is
   4.9 × 10⁻⁵ per deg², below ε, so ε is active at the posterior's thin southern edge. With
   ε = 10⁻⁶ the minimum relative likelihood over mass falls from 0.43 to 0.03 at 1°. ε acts as the
   floor that contract rule 4 forbids in this module's own estimator; that is the D2 argument,
   confirmed numerically.
5. **The un-joined version is noise-dominated and not a usable likelihood.** Only 14 real
   drifters arrive in the window. ESS falls to 0.70, the relative likelihood reaches 21 (1°) and
   583 (0.25°), and the median moves the other way (−2.7 NM). The joining step is what makes
   Davey's method usable. It is also where the unprinted choices sit.
6. **Sensitivities to the unprinted choices** all stay inside "negligible", with medians +1.6 to
   +7.2 NM north. A tight join (50 km, ±15 d) moves it +7.2 NM. R = 100 km gives +1.6 and
   R = 400 km +3.4. None reverses the sign.

## 6. What this does not do

- It does not score the shared impact samples, and it is not an `ocean-model = gdp-empirical`
  likelihood product yet. Doing that needs the impact posterior and the sample interface (§3 of
  the brief).
- It uses no drifter-to-flaperon response correction (flaperon windage), no biofouling
  temperature channel (A2; `sst` is downloaded for it), and no recovery or identification model
  (D5). Each is a stated departure for the module's own estimator, not for this reproduction.
- The bootstrap does not resample the partner pool, so the stated uncertainties are lower bounds.
- All runs were single-threaded on one core while the core run held the heavy-job lock: each
  simulation 2.4–5.6 min, evaluation seconds. Monte Carlo is 40 paths per start in total; more
  would reduce the seed noise in §4 but not the drifter-sample noise.
