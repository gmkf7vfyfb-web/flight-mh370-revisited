Probability density of the impact latitude on the 7th arc. Grey area: the satellite communication and flight data (Davey model with the fuel model; no measurement at 00:19 used). Solid line: the drift model alone (twelve items, two ocean models with equal weight, prior uniform along the arc, smoothed along the arc; the absence of finds on Australian coasts is not used). Hatched area: the two combined. The combined density is the satellite and flight density multiplied by the drift likelihood and normalised; its median is 35.7 degrees south and its mean 34.8, against 36.4 and 35.9 for the satellite and flight data alone. Disagreement between the drift model (two ocean models) and the satellite and flight data: 1.6 standard deviations (1.5 to 1.7 over ocean samples; 1.6 to 1.9 over ocean models and the Australian assumption).
