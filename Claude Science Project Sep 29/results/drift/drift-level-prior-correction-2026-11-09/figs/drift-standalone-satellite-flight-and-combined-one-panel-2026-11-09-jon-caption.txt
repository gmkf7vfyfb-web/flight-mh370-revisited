Probability density of the impact latitude on the 7th arc. Grey area: the satellite communication and flight data (Davey model with the fuel model; no measurement at 00:19 used). Drift model: twelve items on all western Indian Ocean coasts with four detection periods, two ocean models with equal weight unless stated, with a prior that is uniform along the arc, smoothed along the arc by a Gaussian-process fit to the 84 release points. No finds on Australian coasts: detection on populated Australian coasts 0.25 of that on the find coasts. Hatched area: the satellite and flight data combined with the drift model (two ocean models). The combined density is the satellite and flight density multiplied by the drift likelihood and normalised; its median is 35.7 degrees south and its mean 34.8, against 36.4 and 35.9 for the satellite and flight data alone. Medians (degrees south): satellite and flight data 36.4; drift model 30.0; with no finds on Australian coasts 29.5; GlobCurrent only 30.0; GLORYS12 only 29.4; excluding Mossel Bay 32.9. Disagreement between the drift model (two ocean models) and the satellite and flight data: 1.6 standard deviations (1.5 to 1.7 over ocean samples; 1.6 to 1.9 over ocean models and the Australian assumption).
