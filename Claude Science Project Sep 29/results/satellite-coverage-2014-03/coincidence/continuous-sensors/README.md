# Continuously recording satellite sensors: contrail check (PAUSED, 21 Oct 2026)

Status: closed by Pete as a dead end on 21 Oct 2026, filed here in case the line is reopened. Nothing in this folder has been through review. Do not cite it without re-checking.

## Why it was closed
- `contrail-physics/`: ERA5 along the run J posterior routes after 18:22 shows no ice-supersaturated air at FL300-FL400 (posterior P(contrail forms and persists) < 1e-6, raw and Teoh 2022/2024 corrected humidity; stress test RHi/0.8 at most 0.002). A contrail could form only at FL380-FL400, or at FL350 after about 00:00, and would be short-lived (about 0.1 km wide, gone in minutes): narrower than every imager pixel. The Moon (43 % illuminated) was below the horizon at every route point 16:42-00:11, so moonlit day/night band imagery was not possible. Single reanalysis, no ensemble, no radiosonde check.
- This agrees with the null contrail search by NASA (Minnis et al., ATSB 2017 final report pp.126-127).
