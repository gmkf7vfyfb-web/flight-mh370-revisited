Average longitudinal deceleration of calm-water model ditchings, from landing speed v and length of run s. Circles: twelve transport aeroplanes of NACA Report 1347 (Tables 26-37), open for undamaged models and filled for models with scale-strength bottoms; triangles: the same with ditching aids (cargo container, hydro-ski, hydroflap); squares: Lockheed Constellation with Speedpak, NACA RM SL9H05a. Landing speeds 29-84 m/s. The shaded band is the range of mean deceleration used for shallow water entry (0.75-3.0 g); the dashed line marks the lower edge of the sensitivity case (0.45 g). Points are spread vertically within each group for legibility.
