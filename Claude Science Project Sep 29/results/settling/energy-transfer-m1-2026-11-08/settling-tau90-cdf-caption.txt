Weighted cumulative distribution of the mechanical energy-transfer duration at water entry, τ90 (time between 5 % and 95 % of cumulative energy transfer), for (a) the Davey route model with fuel and (b) the waypoint route model. Line style gives the treatment of the 00:19 satellite data: solid, both log-on timings; dashed, timings and both frequencies as recorded; dotted, timings and both frequencies with the Holland start-up offset (17 Hz floor). Black lines use the blend band of 10-30 degrees of flight-path angle between the shallow-entry and steep-entry regimes; grey lines use 5-45 degrees. First-order water-entry model: constant mean deceleration of 0.75-3.0 g for shallow entry, progressive crushing over 0.5-1.0 of the airframe length for steep entry. Weights are those of the end-of-flight stage.
