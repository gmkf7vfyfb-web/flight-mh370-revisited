# Reverse detection in the MH370 hydroacoustic records: literature note

4 November 2026. Scope: the published MH370 hydroacoustic work, and the detection methods from other fields that apply to the data the project holds. Those data are raw IMOS logger records (6 kHz, 8 Hz high-pass, 307 s in every 15 min), the Cape Leeuwin and Diego Garcia South plot traces digitised from Kadri 2024 Fig. 9, and published pick lists. This note does not survey methods for their own sake. Each section ends with what the method gives for these data. The full verified reference list is in the companion files `reverse-methods-references.bib` and `reverse-methods-references.csv` (87 DOI records plus 4 grey-literature items held by the project).

**Bottom line.** No published MH370 study has shown a confirmed impact arrival. The published claims are one single-station candidate at Cape Leeuwin ([Kadri 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-60529-1)) and an acoustic signature near the time of the final transmission whose location is at odds with the satellite analysis (the ATSB statement quoted by [Dall'Osto and Duncan 2023](https://doi.org/10.1121/10.0022760)). For data at envelope resolution and short duty-cycled records, the methods with the best case for transfer are four. First, a local-reference energy detector (CFAR). Second, a persistence statistic that is valid only after decorrelation thinning, or on an empirical null. Third, Page's CUSUM for onset. Fourth, network integration along travel-time hypotheses with time-slide backgrounds, as in gravitational-wave searches and radar track-before-detect. Waveform-level methods (spectral kurtosis, excess power, dispersion ranging) apply to the raw IMOS records only. The significance machinery should be empirical throughout: permutation-safe p-values, tails extrapolated by a generalised Pareto fit and labelled, and look-elsewhere built into the null by taking the maximum over the hypothesis grid in every background trial.

## MH370 and analogue sources

### The MH370 record

The candidate analyses disagree because they rest on different stations, windows and priors, not because they report the same data differently. [Kadri 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-60529-1) examined the Cape Leeuwin and Diego Garcia IMS stations within minutes of the last satellite handshake. That study reports one potential signal, at one of the two stations only. It builds on the inverse-location method of [Kadri et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-14177-3) and on acoustic-gravity-wave propagation over an elastic seabed ([Kadri 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-37626-z)). Kadri's CTBTO SnT 2025 poster (grey literature, held by the project) reports no signal within the ATSB-recommended window. That window is conditioned on the southern satellite posterior, so the poster does not bear directly on a window derived from the Boeing dive cases. An earlier open-data screen ([Stead 2014](https://doi.org/10.2172/1136454)) found only H01, H08 and the Geoscope station AIS openly available. It reported one interesting H01 arrival with interference from an Antarctic ice event, and large repeating signals at H08 that obscure other arrivals; these are the airgun shots of a seismic survey. Curtin CMST (report 2014-30, grey literature) analysed the IMOS loggers and IMS records. The JASA abstracts of [Dall'Osto and Duncan 2023](https://doi.org/10.1121/10.0022760) and [Duncan and Dall'Osto 2023](https://doi.org/10.1121/10.0022761) revisit those detections after the 2019 F-35A case. The first states that, if the acoustic evidence were included, the probable crash area would move to a site roughly 750 km west of the Maldives. The second compares the propagation conditions of the F-35A and MH370 paths. Both are conference abstracts, not peer-reviewed papers. The Bayesian satellite framework against which any acoustic result must be read is in [Davey et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-10-0379-0) and [Ashton et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1017/s037346331400068x).

The Indian Ocean background is dense, and known-event handling is a first-order issue. Over 14 months, three autonomous hydrophones and two permanent stations in the southern Indian Ocean catalogued 11 105 events, with earthquakes down to mb 2.6 ([Royer et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggv178)). Icebergs and ice-shelf failures add sustained tremor and transients ([Talandier et al. 2006](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246x.2006.02911.x)). Mapped T-wave excitation zones in the north-east Indian Ocean show that one earthquake can excite arrivals from wide regions of slope ([Graeber and Piserchia 2004](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246x.2004.02301.x)). For any 10-min arrival window, several unrelated transients are therefore expected. The OHASISBIO picks of 8 March 2014 (Royer 2016 report, held) and the open benchmark of [Raumer et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-egu24-5256) are the local reference catalogues.

### Analogue impulsive sources

Known-time, known-place analogues show that impulsive ocean sources are detected at megametre range, and they set the timing accuracy that a travel-time-only method can expect. Explosions below 40 kg TNT equivalent were detected in the deep sound channel at up to 16 000 km. Their arrival-time mismatches were about 10 s and their azimuth mismatches below 0.5° ([Prior et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1109/joe.2011.2154390)). The F-35A crash of 2019 was recorded at H11 Wake ([Metz et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00024-022-03117-6)), at about 3000 km according to [Duncan and Dall'Osto 2023](https://doi.org/10.1121/10.0022761). The ARA San Juan loss was located from IMS hydroacoustic data ([Nielsen et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00024-020-02445-9); [Vergoz et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00024-020-02625-7); [Dall'Osto 2019](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.5125251)), and further South Atlantic events are documented by [Heyburn et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggaa291). [Brown et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2026.117106) is the closest methodological analogue to this project: a search of IMS hydroacoustic records for signals from bolides of known time and place. Reviews of forensic event monitoring ([Pasyanos et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43017-025-00702-w)) and of natural and man-made sources ([Schwardt et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10712-022-09713-4)) give the source classes that a candidate list must be screened against. Airgun calibration shots in the Indian Ocean ([Blackman et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.1786711); [Tolstoy et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1029/2004gl020234)) describe the repeating-shot structure that dominates the held Diego Garcia South record.

**What this gives for the held data.** Travel-time accuracy of about ±10 s on clear deep-channel paths supports the arc-band mapping. The same accuracy does not support amplitude prediction, which the project no longer uses. One more result transfers directly. A single IMS station can estimate range from modal dispersion if the signal band is at least 5 Hz wide within 5-35 Hz ([Li and Gavrilov 2008](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.2932527)). This needs waveform samples, so it applies only to raw IMOS candidates. It uses propagation for timing (group speed against frequency) and not for amplitude, so it is consistent with the reverse-sensing rule.

## Detection below the noise

### Energy and persistence

The energy detector is the reference test for a signal of unknown form. Its performance depends only on the time-bandwidth product and the signal-to-noise ratio ([Urkowitz 1967](https://doi.org/10.1109/proc.1967.5573)). The practical difficulty is the noise reference. Radar CFAR estimates the noise from reference cells around the test cell, with guard cells between them. Ordered-statistic variants stay robust when other targets fall in the reference cells ([Rohling 1983](https://doi.org/10.1109/taes.1983.309350)). The seismological STA/LTA trigger is the same idea ([Allen 1978](https://doi.org/10.1785/bssa0680051521); [Withers et al. 1998](https://doi.org/10.1785/bssa0880010095)). In a crowded Indian Ocean record, a median and MAD reference ring is the right choice, because a mean reference is biased by neighbouring T-phases.

Pete's persistence idea has a sound statistical basis, but the textbook null distributions assume independence, and envelope samples are not independent. Runs tests for two-state sequences ([Wald and Wolfowitz 1940](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177731909); [Barton and David 1958](https://doi.org/10.2307/2333062)), portmanteau autocorrelation tests ([Ljung and Box 1978](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/65.2.297)) and scan statistics for clusters of points ([Naus 1965](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1965.10480810); [Kulldorff 1997](https://doi.org/10.1080/03610929708831995)) all assume an independent or Poisson null. A plotted or demodulated envelope is smoothed over its own bin width, so successive samples are positively correlated even in pure noise. In this project's unit tests, a binomial sign test on samples thinned to the 1/e correlation lag gave a false-alarm rate of 0.11 at a nominal 0.05. Thinning to the lag where the autocorrelation falls below 0.1 restored the nominal rate. The persistence statistic is therefore kept, with thinning fixed by the measured null autocorrelation. Its decision p comes from the empirical null, not from the binomial formula.

Page's CUSUM combines energy and persistence in one sequential statistic and also gives an onset time ([Page 1954](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/41.1-2.100)). It is minimax-optimal for a change of known size ([Lorden 1971](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177693055)) and is closely related to the sequential probability ratio test ([Wald 1945](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177731118)). The onset time matters more than the detection itself, because onset times map to source times along the arc.

### Waveform-level statistics

Impulsiveness tests need waveform samples. Spectral kurtosis detects non-Gaussian transients band by band ([Antoni 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2004.09.001); [Antoni 2007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2005.12.002)). Kurtosis-based pickers locate seismic onsets ([Saragiotis et al. 2002](https://doi.org/10.1109/tgrs.2002.800438); [Baillard et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120120347)). The Teager-Kaiser operator tracks instantaneous energy at sample resolution ([Kaiser 1990](https://doi.org/10.1109/icassp.1990.115702)). The gravitational-wave excess-power statistic tiles the time-frequency plane and is near-optimal when only the duration and bandwidth ranges are known ([Anderson et al. 2001](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevd.63.042003)). An impact pulse after megametre propagation has an unknown shape but a bounded duration and band, which is exactly this case. All four methods apply to the raw IMOS records and none apply to the digitised plot traces.

### Templates, similarity and subspaces

Waveform correlation lowers detection thresholds by about one magnitude unit when a template from the same source region and path exists ([Gibbons and Ringdal 2006](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246x.2006.02865.x); [Schaff and Waldhauser 2010](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120100042); [Shelly et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05666); [Peng and Zhao 2009](https://doi.org/10.1038/ngeo697)). For MH370 no such template exists. The analogue envelopes (F-35A at Wake, San Juan, depth charges) come from other paths with other dispersion. An earlier project test found that a matched filter on these envelopes is blind to amplitude, so template correlation is used here only together with an energy gate. Fingerprint and autocorrelation searches avoid the need for a template by finding repeats inside the data ([Brown et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1029/2008gl034560); [Yoon et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.1501057)). The matrix profile gives the exact nearest-neighbour distance of every subsequence, and its maxima ("discords") are the most unusual subsequences ([Yeh et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1109/icdm.2016.0179)). We use this in reverse. A window with many near-repeats is a repeating source (airgun, whale call, ice) and is labelled. A window with no near-neighbour is a unique transient, the class an impact would belong to. Subspace detectors generalise templates to a low-rank family of waveforms ([Harris and Paik 2006](https://doi.org/10.2172/898451); [Harris and Dodge 2011](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120100103); [Barrett and Beroza 2014](https://doi.org/10.1785/0220130152)). The Diego Garcia South airgun shots are such a family. This supports Pete's shot-population test: a burst with an added unrelated signal should fall outside the subspace spanned by the other shots.

### Network integration

Every network method that works below single-station detectability integrates evidence along a hypothesis before it thresholds. Seismic back-projection and source scanning stack station characteristic functions along predicted travel times over a space-time grid ([Ishii et al. 2005](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature03675); [Kiser and Ishii 2017](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-earth-063016-015801); [Kao and Shan 2004](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246x.2004.02276.x)). Coherency measures such as semblance ([Neidell and Taner 1971](https://doi.org/10.1190/1.1440186); [Rost and Thomas 2002](https://doi.org/10.1029/2000rg000100)) and progressive multichannel correlation ([Cansi 1995](https://doi.org/10.1029/95gl00468)) need array elements with coherent waveforms, which the data held do not have. Radar track-before-detect does the same with dynamic programming or Hough accumulation over target tracks ([Barniv 1985](https://doi.org/10.1109/taes.1985.310548); [Tonissen and Evans 1996](https://doi.org/10.1109/7.543865); [Carlson et al. 1994](https://doi.org/10.1109/7.250410)). [Davey et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1155/2008/428036) compare several of these algorithms. GNSS acquisition supplies the rule for combining incoherent channels. When phase coherence is lost, energies are summed non-coherently, at a known squaring loss ([Borio and Akos 2009](https://doi.org/10.1109/taes.2009.4805285)). Envelopes at different stations, on different paths, are not phase-coherent. Non-coherent stacking of station scores over the arc grid is therefore the correct model, and waveform cross-correlation between stations is not.

Gravitational-wave searches give the most complete template for significance in a multi-detector coincidence search. The background comes from time slides: one detector's triggers are shifted by more than the physical light-travel time, and the search is rerun ([Abbott et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.116.061102); [Usman et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/33/21/215004)). The method and its limits for networks are set out by [Wąs et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/27/1/015005). [Capano et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevd.96.082002) show that including or excluding the candidate's own triggers from the background changes its significance materially, so both values should be reported. For our short records the number of independent slides is the binding constraint. A 20-min record with a 60 s correlation time supports only a few tens of independent shifts.

### Significance and nulls

Empirical p-values should never be zero ([Phipson and Smyth 2010](https://doi.org/10.2202/1544-6115.1585)). Beyond the null sample, a generalised Pareto tail fit gives an extrapolated p, which must be labelled as extrapolated ([Knijnenburg et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp211); [Pickands 1975](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176343003); [Coles 2001](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4471-3675-0)). Look-elsewhere corrections can be computed analytically ([Gross and Vitells 2010](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-010-1470-8)). It is simpler, and exact for the search as run, to take the maximum over the full hypothesis grid in every null trial. For a ranked list of weak candidates, false-discovery control ([Benjamini and Hochberg 1995](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x)) suits the aim better than family-wise control. Dependent tests can be combined with Brown's method ([Brown 1975](https://doi.org/10.2307/2529826)) or the harmonic mean p ([Wilson 2019](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1814092116)). Here an empirical null of the combined statistic is used instead. When off-window data are scarce, surrogate data ([Theiler et al. 1992](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789%2892%2990102-s); [Schreiber and Schmitz 1996](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.77.635)) and the block bootstrap ([Künsch 1989](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176347265)) preserve the noise spectrum. Change-point segmentation with PELT gives onsets at linear cost ([Killick et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2012.737745)). Machine-learning detectors ([Mousavi and Beroza 2023](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-earth-071822-100323)) are not used, because no labelled impact arrivals exist for these stations. The IDC hydroacoustic pipeline ([Hanson et al. 2001](https://doi.org/10.1007/pl00001190)), T-phase station design ([Okal 2001](https://doi.org/10.1785/gssrl.72.2.186)), autonomous-array T-phase monitoring ([Fox et al. 2001](https://doi.org/10.1029/2000jb900404)) and the original T-phase description ([Tolstoy and Ewing 1950](https://doi.org/10.1785/bssa0400010025)) give the operational context.

## Choice for this data

| Data form held | Methods selected | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Raw IMOS (6 kHz, 34 % duty) | energy excess, persistence, CUSUM, spectral kurtosis with Teager-Kaiser, excess power, change points, discords; dispersion range check for candidates | waveform samples available; two weeks of background days give a large same-station null |
| Kadri digitised plot traces (H01W 30 min, H08S 20 min) | energy excess, persistence, CUSUM, change points, discords, analogue template with energy gate; shot-population test at H08S | envelope only; signed vertices must first be converted to an envelope; the records lie mostly inside the arrival windows, so the null needs form-matched open IMS data from 2015 onward |
| Published picks (Royer, CMST, Kadri Table 1) | pick-cluster scan; coincidence with time slides | point times only |
| Network (all forms) | time-slide coincidence; travel-time stacking over the arc grid | non-coherent integration matches incoherent envelopes on different paths |

The open gaps are three. First, no template exists for an aircraft impact on these paths, so sensitivity can only be measured by injecting synthetic signals into real noise. Second, the Kadri records are short and lie mostly inside the arrival windows, which limits same-record nulls and the number of independent time slides. Third, envelope resolution removes the waveform information that kurtosis, excess-power and dispersion methods need, so Cape Leeuwin and Diego Garcia South can be screened only by envelope methods.
