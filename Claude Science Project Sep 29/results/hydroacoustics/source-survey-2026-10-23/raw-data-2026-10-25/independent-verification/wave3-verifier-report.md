## Errors and issues found outside the check list

1. Wording (F1, task): the triad exposure is the linear mean of three elements (pre-registration R4), not the median. The median would be 0.2855 / 0.0789 Pa^2 s. The 1-s RMS peak ranges in the task (0.31-0.38 / 0.20-0.27 Pa) are 4-40 Hz values. The F-35A note's own table is 4-64 Hz (0.333-0.394 / 0.211-0.291 Pa).
2. Spectral family wording (F-35A coupling rows CV26-CV107): the rows say 'one-pole f^-2'. The module band_fraction is piecewise: flat to the corner, then f^-2, normalised over 1-500 Hz on a 4000-point log grid. A true one-pole form 1/(1+(f/fc)^2) gives eta 3.4 % lower (2.315e-3 against 2.396e-3 at 10 m). Suggest 'flat to 1/tau, f^-2 above (module band_fraction)'.
3. Route lengths (Kadri track, water_route.py): the length is the sum of 8-neighbour grid steps, with no path smoothing. This overstates the route by 1-4 %: SJ182 about +42 km and AB1103 about +125 km against my smoothed routes. The water-path centres are therefore late, and the reported offsets are too negative by about 26 s (SJ182) and 84 s (AB1103). The conclusions do not change.
4. AB1103 expected bearing: the reconciliation table gives 254.56 deg at H08S, from Kadri's S1 position. The published crash location gives 258.40 deg, which is close to Kadri's own Fig. 2 value of 258.47 deg. The S1 position appears to be about 200 km off across the path, although its range is similar (my geodesic 3,190.4 km). This has no effect on the result, because the AB1103 burst has no coherent bearing.
5. SJ182 box peak: my triad-mean 1-s RMS peak is at 08:11:17.73, 0.461 Pa. The track gives 08:11:20.0, 0.415 Pa: 2.3 s and 0.9 dB different. Not diagnosed. The likely cause is a difference in edge taper or envelope hop. This has no effect on any conclusion.
6. H10S1 at the charge: the H10S1 'arrival' (21:09:42.97, implied 1.503 km/s) is a noise maximum, not the arrival. The -27 dB figure for the charge is therefore a lower bound on the H10S1 deficit. The event value (-31.9 dB) is a true arrival comparison.
7. P2 wording (propagation note, section 3): the sentence on the implied window follows the raw Welch comparison. A reader could take it as raw Welch PSD against raw energy. It is raw ESD against Nielsen's digitised PSD. A raw 60-s Welch PSD gives medians of 24.5-75.5 s, depending on the segment length.
8. Value-id collision: three tracks write coupling rows from CV26: F-35A (CV26-CV107), Transair 810 (CV26-CV35) and propagation (CV26-CV31). The importer must renumber. The F-35A note says this; the other two notes do not.
9. Minor: the Transair note gives the H11N - H11S pulse lag as 72-73 s. I get 73.3 s and 71.5 s.
