## How the persistence (successive-sample) test was defined and how it performed

Definition as pre-registered (reverse-sensing specification v1.0, 4 Nov 2026, detector S2 'sample-to-sample persistence', code reverse_detectors.py 1.0.0, functions persistence, decorrelation_lag, runs_test_z). It was Pete's detector 2 (4 Nov 2026: 'sample-to-sample correlation/persistence with binomial/Poisson statistics').

Data form. The test runs on a level envelope y = 20 log10(envelope) at 10 samples/s, never on raw plot vertices. Kadri vertices alternate top/bottom of the trace (lag-1 correlation below -0.8 by construction), so they are first converted to an envelope: maximum |x| in each 0.1 s bin. Raw IMOS records use band envelopes (mode max) in 8-20, 20-40, 40-100 and 8-40 Hz.

Statistic. d_i = y_i - median(y in the ring). The ring is the non-overlapping windows within max(120 s, 6L) on each side, outside a guard of max(10 s, L) (deviation D10: S1 ring geometry). Thin the samples by lag tau, where tau is the first lag at which the null autocorrelation of y falls below 0.1. In the window, K = number of positive d among the n thinned samples; p_bin = P(Binomial(n, 0.5) >= K); decision statistic T2 = -log10 p_bin. Windows L = 10 s and 30 s, step L/4. Secondary labels: Wald-Wolfowitz runs z on the unthinned signs (negative = clustering) and the lag-1 autocorrelation r1 of d inside the window. The decision p is empirical against null windows of the same form (N1 same record off-window, N2 IMOS background days, N3 open IMS in plot form, N4 surrogates); p_bin is reported only as a nominal value. Candidate rule: local empirical p <= 0.05. Combined form S3: F = -2[ln p1 + ln p2] with the energy test S1, null taken empirically.

Why thinning at the 0.1 lag. On synthetic noise with a 1 s correlation time the false-alarm rate at nominal 0.05 was 0.22 with no thinning, 0.11 thinning at the 1/e lag and 0.049 at the 0.1 lag. Measured thinning lags on the data: lag 1 at Cape Leeuwin and lag 7 at Diego Garcia South (Kadri traces; memory mem_4d9c990a1eef gives the lags without units, read here as 0.1 s envelope samples); 8-13 s at Scott Reef 3250 and Perth Canyon 3376, where a 10 s window holds 1 sample, so S2 has no power there.

Same statistic inside the shot-population test (S7): 'persistence of the template residual (sign-count z after thinning)' is one of eight per-cycle features of the Diego Garcia South airgun cycles.

Performance - synthetic reference (spec section 11, 1 s rise / 5 s decay, local p <= 0.05, 40 injections per SNR): P_D at 10-s SNR -9/-6/-3/0/+3 dB: persistence 0.00/0.05/0.35/0.72/1.00; combined 0.08/0.15/0.48/0.95/1.00; energy alone 0.05/0.08/0.15/0.72/1.00 (about 3 dB gain for the combination).

Performance - null calibration on real noise (design value far below 5 %). Station-level false alarms per 10 s look: Cape Leeuwin 8 Mar off-window trace under N4 surrogates: persistence 19 %, combined 16 %; Cape Leeuwin open data in plot form: persistence 15 %, combined 15 %; Diego Garcia South open data: 4-7 % for all four detectors (reverse-screening README section 3; sensitivity CSV far_station_global 0.193 / 0.151 / 0.037). N3 failed its form check (lag-1 ACF 0.19 vs 0.01-0.05), so N4 was primary and proved anti-conservative. The form-matched block-bootstrap null (8 Mar off-window 440 s, mean block 80 s) gives per-look false alarms at nominal 5 % of 0.052 (10 s) and 0.073 (30 s) for persistence, 0.064 and 0.073 for combined (20 s blocks: persistence 0.087 and 0.091) (kadri-form-matched-null-calibration.csv).

Performance - sensitivity (station-level p <= 0.05, injections into real null data): SNR50 persistence -5.9 dB and combined -5.6 dB at Cape Leeuwin (off-window host); -4.5 / -4.1 dB on Cape Leeuwin open data; +3.2 / -3.8 dB at Diego Garcia South; > +12 dB on all raw IMOS loggers (persistence local SNR50 +1.5 dB at Portland 3274, +10.1 dB at Portland 3275).

Performance - detections. 54 persistence candidates at local p <= 0.05 (Cape Leeuwin 27, Diego Garcia South 9, SWAMS3 5, Portland 3274 5, SSEIR 4, Portland 3275 3, Perth Canyon 3315 1). Strongest: Cape Leeuwin Kadri trace onset 00:36:07.5, 30 s window, T2 14.55, local p 6.7e-7, station p 0.0002 (the floor of 5,000 draws under N4). The association run identified this onset as the Royer 2016 Fig. 5 pick bar for event B (association 0.94). Next: Cape Leeuwin 00:46:56.9 (10 s), station p 0.021. Under the form-matched null the three strongest Cape Leeuwin groups (00:36:07.4, 00:51:58.3, 00:54:27.4) sit at station-level 0.011 and coincide with Royer events B, A and 5. No persistence candidate was removed and none is a detection: no network result reached p 0.05. In S7 no shot cycle was significant (minimum global p 0.17 panel d, 0.53 panel e). No persistence-type test ran before 4 Nov 2026; the October shot-template test used shape correlation with a template, not successive-sample statistics.
