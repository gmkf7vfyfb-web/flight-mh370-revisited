Figure X. Source position implied if the Cape Leeuwin arrival at 00:49:58 UTC and the Diego Garcia South arrival at 01:10:15 UTC came from one impact. (a) Monte Carlo source positions (4,000 draws per case) with 50 % (solid) and 95 % (dotted) error ellipses, for the Cape Leeuwin bearing corrected for the array position (246.8°, black) and as published (260.4°, grey). The solid curve is the 7th arc; the dashed curves are 150 NM either side of it. (b) Mean ground speed needed to reach the source from the nearest point of the 7th arc between 00:19:29 UTC and the impact time; the dashed line marks 500 kt. Bearing standard deviation 1.26°; arrival times uniform from 20 s before onset to the envelope peak; sound speed uniform within physical bounds from WOA23 March climatology.
