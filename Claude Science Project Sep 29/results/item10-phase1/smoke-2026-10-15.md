## 2. Why: the split-half test (pass 0, 100,000 uniform parents, 32 descents each)

G_A sums weight × likelihood over a parent's first 4 children (16 descents), and G_B over the other 4. Given the
parent they are independent, so Σ G_A G_B estimates Σ ℓ² without bias, and (Σ G)² / (4 Σ G_A G_B) estimates the
effective parents of the TRUE parent-level likelihood ℓ. That is the ceiling any hand-off proposal could reach.

- **Reading.**
  - For every arm that scores a 00:19 BFO, the two halves are uncorrelated (|corr| ≤ 0.03). The top parents of
    one half are not the top parents of the other (overlap at chance).
  - The parent-level likelihood is therefore close to FLAT across the 00:11 parents: estimated ceiling 40,000–89,000
    of 100,000. The observed 161–183 (both raw) and 651–741 (both with startup offset) come from which DESCENT
    happened to hit, not from which parent was handed off.
  - The ceiling estimates are noisy, because few parents have hits in both halves. The direction is not noisy:
    halves are uncorrelated, and the overlap is at chance.
- **Conclusion.** The both-burst arms are **descent-limited, not parent-limited.**
  - This reverses the reading "about ten of 100,000 cruise states can produce the 00:19 pair; the constraint is
    the 00:11 hand-off" (architecture.md about line 4613).
  - It agrees with settling's diagnosis (`results/settling-h1h2-estimability.md`): the descent proposal puts
    little weight on the push-over the BFO pair needs.
  - Request 20 failed for this reason. g from 32 pilots was noise, so drawing parents in proportion to it only
    added weight variance.
- **What follows.**
  1. **Request 20 is NOT for run J** (no pilot time).
  2. **The remedy belongs to end of flight:** more descents per parent and/or an aimed push-over proposal with an
     exact correction. Effective parents should grow about in proportion to the descents per parent while far
     below the ceiling. For example, 32 → 320 descents gives about 1,600–1,800 for both raw, if the hit rate is
     the same. That is a prediction for end of flight to test, not a measurement.
  3. The 00:11 hand-off size (100k) is not the bottleneck for these arms.
- **Scope.** Repro stratum only, 2 seeds, end of flight's run I recipe at HEAD (e069ff8 / d55e75c). The other
  strata were NOT ASSESSED. They share the same descent stage, so the same result is expected.
