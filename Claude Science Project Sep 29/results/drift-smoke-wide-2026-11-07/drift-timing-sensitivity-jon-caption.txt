Figure Y. Effect of the time between arrival on a coast and discovery on the debris-only estimate. (a) Posterior mean latitude over the whole tested area; (b) over the 22 source points within 150 NM of the median impact track; negative values are south. The first five rows apply one exponential delay to all nine finds. The shaded rows use a mixture of delays in which the flaperon and the Mossel Bay engine cowling, both found with fresh marine growth, take the short delay. The last row uses the second beaching date of the cowling. (c) Arrival times on the South African south coast, relative to the first sighting of the cowling on 23 December 2015, for simulated cowlings released at three points near the track; both current models pooled.
