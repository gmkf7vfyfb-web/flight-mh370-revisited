Figure X. Impact probability from the floating-debris evidence alone, with no constraint from the satellite data. Rows: ocean current models GlobCurrent and GLORYS12. (a) The nine confirmed or almost-certain finds; Australian coasts not used. (b) As (a), with no debris found in Australia, where the probability of finding and reporting a piece is 0.25 of that on the coasts where debris was found. (c) As (b), with that ratio equal to 1. Shading is the posterior probability per block of about 300 × 300 km, with a uniform prior over the tested area (46–14° S, 64–112° E, outlined); the cross is the posterior mean; the dashed line is the median impact track from the satellite and flight data, shown for reference only. Results come from 24,000 simulated pieces per source point and model and are not converged.
