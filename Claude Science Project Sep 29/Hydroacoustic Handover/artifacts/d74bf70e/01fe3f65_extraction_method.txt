PROCEDURE
  1. Appendix A tables. Every row was transcribed from the text layer and then checked character by character
     against 300-dpi renders. I cropped each table half and read them visually. As a second check, each table
     was re-serialised and its whitespace-stripped string compared with the text layer. Table I, 2, 3, 4 and 5
     all matched exactly.
  2. Decimal degrees: lat = -(deg + min/60), because the column headers say lat (S); lon = +(deg + min/60) (E).
     They are rounded to 6-7 decimals, and the printed strings are kept alongside.
     Dates come from year + JD (2001 and 2003 are not leap years).
  3. Appendix B. The per-panel annotations (predicted/observed arrival, ND marker, back-azimuth arrows) are inside
     raster figures and are not in the text layer. All 78 panel-station cells (26 events x 3 stations) were read
     from 300-dpi renders. Each was re-read on cropped annotation strips (crops/ann2_p27..p33) for digit
     certainty. ND-marker and arrow presence were read from full-page renders.
  4. Receiver observations were coded under the conservative rule: status comes ONLY from an explicit statement
     (text, caption, or an Appendix B observed/ND annotation). Silence is coded not_reported. Status vocabulary:
       detected; detected_questionable_pick (numeric pick printed with '?', no ND marker);
       not_detected; not_detected_with_tentative_pick (ND marker drawn AND numeric pick with '?');
       not_assessed_data_truncated; not_reported; ambiguous_as_printed.
     SIO, LLNL and other analyses are kept as separate rows. They are never merged into one verdict.
  5. Transmission loss. The report prints NO numeric observed TL in its text. The only measured TL is Figure 23,
     a 351x458 px grayscale raster. It was read by eye from a 600-dpi render (about ±2 dB, ±2 Hz) and
     cross-checked with a pixel trace of the darkest curve. The axes were calibrated from the frame lines and
     the tick-label centres (36 px per 10 dB; 2.88 px per Hz). Coverage is partial: about 70-80% of columns,
     and 51/55-60 Hz is missed. All TL numbers are labelled figure-read or pixel-traced. None is a printed value.
  6. Appendix B relative-timing check (appendixB_timing_consistency.csv). Within a site, the differences between
     predicted arrival times were compared with Table 3-5 origin-time differences.

CROSS-CHECK RESULTS
  Shot lines (14 segments) and all 35 non-airgun events in the recovered 2026-09-27 bundle agree with this
  re-extraction on every coordinate, depth, interval and time. The only differences are seconds padding and
  the earlier bundle's field name 'shot_ranges', which actually holds SHOT NUMBERS. Receiver observations:
  see receiver_observations_diff_vs_drive.csv. In short, 9 Drive rows (7, 15, 19, 24-29) give a status, station or range
  that UCRL-TR-207323 does not support, and row 23 is less specific than the report. A further 8 rows agree on
  status but cite features not named in UCRL (sph6 4168/1960 km and sph6 at H01; sph3/sph4/sph2 at
  Diego Garcia; air6 not detected at H08S; 'Chagos Bank'; 'Saya de Malha Bank'). The Drive copy has no
  Appendix B (LLNL) per-station results.
