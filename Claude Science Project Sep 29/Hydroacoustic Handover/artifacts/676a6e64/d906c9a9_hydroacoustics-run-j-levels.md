## 1. The result in words

At the F-35A upper anchor of the coupling efficiency (η = 2.1e-4), under the primary option (00:19 held out, D0′, dive probability p = 0.5), the **median simulated impact sits below the background noise median at every receiver**: by 8.7 dB at Cape Leeuwin H01W, 11.6 dB at Diego Garcia South H08S, 5.8 dB at Perth Canyon 3315, 10.9 dB at Perth Canyon 3376, 7.5 dB at Portland 3275, 19 dB at Portland 3274, 29 dB at H08N (98 % of paths blocked) and more than 200 dB at Scott Reef 3250 (93 % blocked). The share of impact mass whose predicted 5–40 Hz level clears the **envelope matched filter** threshold (SNR ≥ 7 dB, P_D = 0.5 at α = 0.01 on real 3376 background; a model-matched upper bound) is 8 % at H01W, 14 % at 3315, 11 % at 3275, and below 0.5 % everywhere else; **no receiver has any mass above the baseline STA/LTA threshold (15 dB)** at the anchor. Taking the best unblocked receiver per impact, 14 % of the mass is detectable by the matched filter and 0 % by the baseline detector. Five times the anchor (η = 1e-3) raises the matched-filter shares to 23 % (H01W), 12 % (H08S), 30 % (3315), 15 % (3376), 25 % (3275); 30 % at the best receiver; the baseline detector then reaches 2 % at H01W and 12 % at 3315. The η at which half the impact mass would be detectable (η₅₀, matched filter) is 7.7e-3 at H01W, 1.5e-2 at H08S, 4.0e-3 at 3315, 1.3e-2 at 3376 and 6.0e-3 at 3275: 20–70 times the F-35A anchor. The Holland data treatments (D3, D4) pull the impacts south and east and roughly double the shares (best-receiver matched-filter share 19 % and 31 % at the anchor); p moves them by ±2 percentage points.

**Power screen (pre-registered rule: matched-filter share at η = 1e-3 ≥ 0.05 KEEP, 0.01–0.05 MARGINAL, < 0.01 DROP; identical verdict in all 12 option × p cells): KEEP H01W, H08S, 3315, 3376, 3275; DROP H08N (98 % of paths blocked, no RAM delta), 3274 (RAM-corrected table 19 dB below noise at the median), 3250 (93 % blocked; open paths over the North West Shelf at TL > 160 dB).** DROP means the receiver cannot be expected to have recorded the impact under this model; its rows stay in every table and nothing is deleted. A non-detection at a DROP receiver carries no information (work-order rule).

So, in words: on this chain, the impact is expected to have been **at or below the noise** at every station unless the coupling efficiency was several times the F-35A value; only the dispersion-aware matched filter, not a conventional energy detector, has any expectation of seeing a minority (8–14 %) of the feasible impacts at H01W and the Perth Canyon / Portland loggers; and the stations that would see most are 3315 and 3275, whose transmission-loss tables rest on the extrapolated RAM correction (G-TL1) and are the least trustworthy in the set. The two Perth Canyon loggers, 100 m apart, differ by a factor 40 in detectable share (3315 14 %, 3376 0.3 %) for no physical reason: that is the size of the TL-table uncertainty at the seabed receivers (G-TL1/G-TL2), and it is the first thing to fix before any IMOS statistic is composed.

## 2. Inputs and method (as pre-registered; §8 lists the deviations)

- **Impact set and weights.** Run J, 74 seeds, 313,599,968 rows, plus the northern supplement (1,277,785 technique ≥ 1 rows). Weights are the windows track's (`run_j_windows.ln_weights`, `p_factor`, imported unchanged): EoF's two-factor table, "total q = 1/3 (C2 silent)", survival factor, strata pooled by p_core/K (waypoints 0.9982), supplement share s_D (D0′ 0.126, D2 0.111, D3 0.106, D4 0.105), dive probability p ∈ {0, 0.5, 1}. Levels and windows therefore share one weighting; the family shares (A1 0.167, A2 0.338, B 0.494 under D0′) reproduce the windows note to three decimals.
- **Subsample.** 2,000,000 weighted draws with replacement per option × p (12 keys), allocated to seeds in proportion to p_core/K (about 73k per waypoints seed, 60–150 per seed of the other strata), n_supp = round(2M s_D) from the supplement; per-draw weights carry the seed's share of the mass under that key. Kish ESS of the subsample 1.80–1.93 M per key (the underlying posterior ESS is the windows note's: 1.34 M under D0′/p0.5, 107k D2, 5.7k D3, 25k D4; the subsample cannot add information beyond those). rng streams fixed in the pre-registration; all 74 seed files verified loadable with the expected draw counts.
- **Source model** (the per_impact_lnl / lhyd chain, re-implemented as in the TL-grid evidence view): acoustic energy η E with E = `energy_transferred_j` = total contact kinetic energy (checked equal to ½ m v² to 3e-7; an upper anchor); SE₁ = η E ρc/(2π) Pa²·s; one-pole energy spectrum flat to 1/τ and f⁻² above, normalised over 1–500 Hz, S_b per third octave; τ_ac = τ₉₀ from `tau90_v1.npz` clamped to 0.05–10 s (k = 1, primary; 5–7 % of mass clamped); sensitivities k ∈ {0.5, 1, 2} drawn per row and the scenario τ ~ log-U(0.05, 10 s). Source depth 10 m (grid reference). Attitude: `bank_source = 1` (wings level by model assumption) holds 41–54 % of the mass per key; the chain has no attitude dependence, so it is a label only. η grid {1e-2, 1e-3, 2.1e-4, 1e-4, 1e-5, 1e-6}; η shifts every level by 10 log₁₀ η, so the chain is evaluated once and shifted.
- **Transmission loss.** The block-2 whole-arc grid (`results-data/tl_grid_run_j`, 41–20 °S), offset-0 line, linear in latitude, clamped at the ends, blocked band → zero energy; IMS H01W/H08S raw KRAKEN minus the imp500 RAM delta (lhyd rule); H08N raw KRAKEN (no delta: upper bound on loss); IMOS 3315/3376/3274/3275 RAM-corrected table (rule 2, extrapolated for 10–33 % of open rows, G-TL1); 3250 uncorrected. Ridge-source rows (32.0–31.5 °S) kept as flagged; `adiabatic_suspect` paths carry TL as an upper bound on loss, so the levels here are lower bounds before C2. Cross-arc offset not used (latitude only, as per_impact_lnl): 8.5 % of D0′ mass lies beyond ±60 NM (G-TL-X).
- **Received level.** Per band L_b = 10 log₁₀(SE₁ S_b 10^(−TL_b/10) / T_eff / 10⁻¹²) dB re 1 µPa, the 1 s-equivalent band SPL with T_eff = 2.683 s; total L = 10 log₁₀ Σ_b 10^(L_b/10) over the ten 5–40 Hz third octaves. SNR = L + G − N₅₀: G = 4.8 dB triad gain at IMS; N₅₀ the module's 5–40 Hz 1 s background medians (IMOS imos2b: 3315 97.2, 3376 96.2, 3250 107.8, 3274 102.9, 3275 94.5 dB; IMS = 3376 proxy + Blackman App. B offset: H01W 101.7, H08S 95.6, H08N 90.2 dB, digitised, May–June 2003).
- **Thresholds** (weak-signal track, `hydroacoustics-weak-signal-methods.md`): matched filter 7 dB (M1b envelope, 6.9–8.0 dB over τ at α 0.01), baseline detector 15 dB (STA/LTA 13.4–15.7 dB). Both are model-matched upper bounds measured on 3376 background; at the other receivers they are transferred, not measured (3274's own matched-filter SNR₅₀ is 2.3 dB and 3250's 25.9 dB in that trial — the transfer is conservative at Portland and optimistic at Scott Reef, which is blocked anyway).
- **Kadri's chain** (Refresh v2 §5): 5 Hz high-pass × 2–40 Hz band-pass, taken as 4th-order Butterworth (order assumed); per-band mean |H|² = 0.51 (5 Hz), 0.86, 0.97, 0.99, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 0.98, 0.88, 0.49 (40 Hz), applied to signal and noise alike.

## 6. Math check (brief Refresh §10)

TL at H01W, 10 Hz, 10 m source, interpolated linearly in latitude on the arc line between the 37.5 and 37.0 °S grid rows, minus the imp500 RAM delta: **127.853 dB**.
