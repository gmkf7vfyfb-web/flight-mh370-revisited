+### 1a. AF447 → H10S (2,209.9 km): Q1, coupling compared with the F-35A
+
+Kadri's AF447 panel is an **upper limit only**: 0.914 Pa at SNR 1.32 (module interim), so every Δ below is an
+**upper bound**. Δ_ratio = 10 log10[(p_e/p_F)² (E_F/E_e) G_F/G_e], over the τ grid 0.05–10 s × z_s {2, 10, 30} m,
+with band-fraction slope 2.
+
+| Energy convention | Δ_ratio upper bound, median (range) | per station H11N / H11S, median | secondary vs η_cal (R = 3.013 /s), median (range) |
+|---|---|---|---|
+| total KE (E_e = 625 MJ) | **≤ +17.0 dB** (+16.0 to +20.7) | +17.9 / +16.1 | ≤ +21.6 dB (+20.6 to +25.3) |
+| vertical KE (E_e = 315 MJ) | **≤ +20.0 dB** (+19.0 to +23.7) | +20.9 / +19.1 | ≤ +24.5 dB (+23.6 to +28.3) |
+
+- **What it says.** At the F-35A's coupling per joule, the model predicts a peak of **0.13 Pa** at H10S (range
+  0.08–0.14), against a panel noise p99 of 0.69 Pa. Kadri's panel could show a peak at SNR 2 only if AF447 coupled
+  **about 20.6 dB more per joule** than the F-35A (19.6–24.3; vertical KE: 23.6). The non-detection is therefore
+  consistent with F-35A coupling. Kadri's "detection" of AF447 would need about 100× the F-35A coupling.
+- **Q1 verdict (pre-registered thresholds):** **not testable on AF447.** An upper bound of +16 to +24 dB cannot
+  separate |Δ| ≤ 6, 6–10 or > 10 dB. It only excludes coupling more than about 16 dB (total KE) above the F-35A's. The
+  convention comparison (total vs vertical) is not decidable from one upper bound. Yemenia is not scorable, so A and
+  B together cannot give a Q1 verdict in stage 1.
+- **Why G_e is small** (TL 17–18 dB more than the F-35A paths after band weighting, though 1,100 km shorter). On
+  **86 %** of the AF447 path there is no depth excess: the critical depth for the warm surface (c ≈ 1,541 m/s) is
+  absent or below the seabed. Energy from a near-surface source therefore reaches H10S only by bottom-interacting
+  paths. AF447's TL rises above 16 Hz (Panel A), where the F-35A's falls. **This result depends on the bottom
+  model** (hard half-space, 0.5 dB/λ), which is the part the audit found not calibrated in slope (+10.4 / +7.4 dB/oct).
+- **What it constrains:** one broadband number (Kadri's 2–40 Hz, high-passed at 5 Hz) from one hydrophone. It gives
+  no band-by-band level, slope or shape, so C1 cannot be applied. Source depth matters by only about 1 dB in Δ
+  (z_s 30 m is lowest), because the F-35A ratio cancels most of the z_s dependence. τ moves Δ by up to 3.7 dB, at
+  τ < 0.25 s.

+### 1b. San Juan charge → H10N (5,989.0 km): Q2, PROVISIONAL
+
+- **The prereg's Q2 test cannot be scored yet.** The absolute test needs a published explosive source spectrum,
+  cited with pages; none is verifiable (Gaspin & Shuler 1971 is blocked on Pete). The fallback, the station difference
+  H10N − H04S, needs the H04S section, which was not built. **No C1/C2 verdict is given.**
+- **Descriptive, PROVISIONAL.** The implied source spectrum is the observed level (noise removed in power) plus the
+  modelled TL at 33 m (Panel C). It is 208.9–214.9 dB re 1 µPa² m²/Hz. Least-squares slope −1.83 ± 0.33 dB per octave;
+  detrended RMS 0.89 dB; flat to within 1 dB from 5 to 12.5 Hz, then falling.
+  - The ordinate is Nielsen's PSD. An absolute comparison needs Nielsen's analysis window before any unit conversion.
+  - A source-spectrum shape will be checked against this when a cited model exists. The smoothness (RMS < 1 dB) is
+    the only signal that the band-to-band TL shape is not grossly wrong on this path.
+- **Rio Grande Rise.** At 2,590–2,672 km the profile depth falls to 701 m (5 km median; track 674 m). The modes kept
+  through the whole path are only **2–4 below 10 Hz** (15 at 40 Hz), so adiabatic mode stripping sets the
+  low-frequency TL. The 5–10 Hz values are the least reliable, and an N×2D treatment (module development) could move
+  them in either direction.
+- **What it constrains, when scorable:** propagation from a 33 m source, the only check available in 5–12.5 Hz (SNR
+  4–15 dB in every band). It does **not** test the coupling of a surface impact (2–10 m, impulsive airframe source).
+  It is a moderate-depth explosive with a bubble pulse, so it constrains the TL and the near-surface/channel coupling
+  for z_s ≈ 30 m only.

+**Finding: the EoF τ does not move the module's detectability.** The per-row change is below 0.45 in lnL at the 1st and
+99th percentiles in every seed. The share of penalised rows rises by 0.1 percentage point. This is not because the two
+τ sets agree; they do not (Panel A):
+- EoF: q05/q50/q95 = 0.125 / 3.7 / 10.6 s, bimodal (dives at 0.08–0.2 s, ditchings at 1.6–10 s);
+- scenario: log-uniform 0.05–10 s.
+
+The change is small for two structural reasons in the module's chain:
+1. `band_fraction` is normalised over 1–500 Hz (audit F7), so S_b is constant for τ > 1 s (Panel B). Most EoF rows are
+   there.
+2. η_cal(τ) is inverted from the F-35A **at each τ**, so S_b(τ) cancels except through the difference in TL spectral
+   shape between the F-35A path and each impact path.
+
+So **τ is nearly a cancelled nuisance in the current chain.** A τ-dependence will appear only once coupling is
+calibrated on more than one τ (G-H1). This is a property of the model, which the module should state. It is not
+evidence that τ is unimportant physically.
+
+Declared assumption: the mechanical energy-transfer duration is used **as** the acoustic source duration. The τ ruling
+keeps these distinct, and the mapping is the module's to make.

+## COVERAGE
+
+**(a) Feasible set.**
+- Calibration events: deep-water (> 1,000 m) near-surface sources at IMS triads with published kinematics or yield.
+  The prereg lists A (AF447), B (Yemenia 626) and C (San Juan charge). Shallow-sea events are excluded with a stated
+  reason (later stage, RAM).
+- MH370 impact duration: water-entry from ditching (seconds) to steep dive (about 0.1 s), and in-flight breakup (not
+  yet a modelled end state, G-H2/G13).
+
+**(b) Model's reach.**
+- Adiabatic 2-D along the geodesic. It **cannot** represent blockage or diffraction round the Rio Grande Rise
+  (San Juan) or Saya de Malha Bank (Yemenia). This is a **reach gap**, open, with N×2D as the module's next method
+  (ruling 6, 00:50 −0600).
+- The bottom model is a single hard half-space, so the AF447 result is conditional on it (**gap**, labelled).
+- z_s grid {2, 10, 30} m (+33 m here).
+- The τ lookup grid is 0.05–10 s. **5.9–6.1 % of run C rows have τ > 10 s and are clamped** (reach gap, labelled).
+- S_b is degenerate above 1 s (F7, **reach gap**).
+- Coupling has no angle, speed or breakup dependence (G-H1, open).
+
+**(c) Proposal coverage.**
+- Calibration: one upper limit (AF447) and one PROVISIONAL spectrum (San Juan, H10N only). Q1 has **no estimable
+  verdict**; Q2 is **not scorable**.
+- τ sensitivity: every row of all 32 seeds (102.4 M), unweighted.
+  - **ESS per 00:19 option and per family (A1/A2/B) was not computed here.** That is a gap in this note; the
+    composer's pass 1 is where the weighted version belongs.

+  - Rows above 212 m/s (G13) are included, not split out.
