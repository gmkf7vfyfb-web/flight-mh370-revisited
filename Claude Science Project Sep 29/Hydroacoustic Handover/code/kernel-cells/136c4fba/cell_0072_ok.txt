+## 3. What is and is not testable on the data we hold
+
+**One correction to the work order.** The digitised H08S *record* does not end at 01:18:29; the airgun
+*shot train* does. Panel e runs to 01:19:58.998 with 8,973 vertices at a median native spacing of
+0.049 s. The last closed airgun cycle starts at 01:18:19. The consequence is specific rather than
+general:
+
+- The two cycle statistics that need a *closed* cycle — shot energy and inter-shot gap power — are
+  computable in the text window (7 shots) and **not computable** in the caption window. Six rows are
+  reported as not computable for that reason. Nothing coarser was substituted.
+- The four statistics that need only a shot and its shoulders — shape correlation against the local
+  stacked template, the leading, trailing and tail shoulder residuals, and the principal-component
+  outlier score — are computable in **both** windows, because shot peaks continue to 01:19:09. The
+  caption window holds 2 of them.
+- At Perth Canyon there is no repeating airgun train, so a local stacked *shot* template does not exist
+  and the shape and shoulder statistics are undefined there. The defined analogue, a matched filter
+  against the committed real transient T_C2, was added as D4 and is new in this run.
+
+**The stop condition was not triggered.** At 40 Hz each shot window carries 360 samples over 9 s, and
+the native vertex spacing of 0.049 s (95th percentile 0.190 s) sits well inside the 0.25 s envelope
+smoothing the statistic uses. Resolution is adequate and is recorded per panel in `summary.json`.
+
+**Perth Canyon coverage.** Logger 3315 has zero coverage in both windows, confirming the earlier
+finding rather than assuming it. Logger 3376 covers 26.0 s of each window — the full 95 per cent window
+in both cases — in recording `531A6929.DAT`.
+
+### The two rows that reach 0.05
+
+**Perth Canyon 3376, caption window, band-power exceedance D2: 9.88 dB, p = 0.0196 against a
+7,046-realisation window null, peaking at 00:53:27.853, inside the window; SNR 8.73 dB, maximum 1 s
+5–40 Hz level 104.94 dB re 1 uPa.** This is the loudest band-power moment of the whole 307 s recording
+and it falls inside the 26 s predicted window. Three things argue against reading it as an arrival.
+First, the STA/LTA energy-ratio detector sees nothing there: D1 peaks 0.7 s away at p = 0.432. Second,
+and more telling, the matched filter against T_C2 gives **p = 1.000** at that time — the excursion
+matches the shape of a real transient *worse* than every background window tested, which is what a rise
+in band-limited noise looks like, not an impulsive arrival. Third, it belongs to the caption option,
+which §4 shows is not the time Kadri's own figure carries. The earlier item-2b pass scored the same
+9.884 dB at p = 0.16 on a 307 s window; both are correct for their own window, and the pre-registered
+one here is the 26 s window, in which the same value is rarer.
+
+**H08S, caption window, shape correlation rho: 0.385, p = 0.0459 on the pooled d+e null** (p = 0.0816 on
+the primary panel-e null). In independent-block units this is the 2nd most extreme 2-shot block of
+about 24, and it rests on 2 shots. The direction is right — low shape correlation is the anomaly — but
+the sample is two cycles and the pooled null is the secondary.
+
+In the text window, on the panel-e null, four primary statistics are each the most extreme 7-shot block
+in the panel — inter-shot gap power, shape correlation, trailing-shoulder residual and the
+principal-component score, all at p = 0.159. Panel e holds only 6.3 independent blocks of that size, so
+"most extreme in the panel" and p = 0.16 are the same statement. The four are also not independent:
+they all respond to the energy structure of the same cycles. Four agreeing statistics here are not four
+pieces of evidence.

+## 4. Amendment A1 — Kadri's two times, resolved at Cape Leeuwin
+
+Panel c of his figure 9 starts at 00:47:00 and runs 600 s, so it contains both candidate times. This is
+a two-point comparison between times fixed by his paper, not a search.
+
+**The panel maximum of the 2–20 Hz envelope is at 00:52:04** — 1.25 s from the text time, 145.75 s from
+the caption time. His text calls 00:52:03 the figure 9c peak, and the figure agrees with the text.
+Ranked against all positions of the same ±3 s window across the panel, in independent-window units
+(about 99 in the panel):
+
+| statistic | 00:52:03, text | rank | 00:54:30, caption | rank |
+|---|---|---|---|---|
+| envelope peak, Pa | 1.168 | **1 of 99** | 0.866 | 3.9 |
+| RMS over ±1 s, Pa | 0.250 | 4.8 | 0.267 | 3.2 |
+| STA/LTA maximum | 3.78 | **1 of 94** | 2.81 | 3.0 |
+| matched filter vs T_C2 | 0.0774 | **1 of 95** | 0.0531 | 24.5 |
+
+Three of four favour the text time and on three of four it is the largest independent excursion in the
+panel. Under the rule fixed before computing: **the text time, 00:52:03, is the time his figure shows**,
+and 00:54:30 reads as a transcription error. Two qualifications. The caption time is not empty — a real
+excursion sits there, third or fourth largest on amplitude though about 25th on waveform match — so a
+reader could defend it as a second, smaller arrival, and the question still goes to Kadri as a
+confirmation rather than an open ask. And panel c is clipped at the plot's ±1 Pa rail, so every
+amplitude is a lower bound, although no sample in either ±3 s window is itself at the rail.
+
+**What A1 does not say.** It identifies which time his figure plots. It is not a detection of an impact
+signal and does not bear on the MH370 hypothesis in either direction.
+
+The digitised band is 2–20 Hz, not the 2–40 Hz of the earlier H01W level convention: the vertex rate
+limits the usable band to 20 Hz. The 2–40 Hz label is reachable only on the 500 Hz IMOS recordings.

+## 5. Likelihood ratio, and why it is one
+
+The conditional fixes the source **location** from Kadri's bearing, so the arrival windows and every
+measured p-value are independent of the dive probability p. The dive probability enters only through
+the predicted received level, by way of the impact energy distribution. Reweighting `run-i-dive` by 2p
+and 2(1-p) on `latent:pushover_to_surface` gives a median energy transferred of 1.23 GJ at p = 0.5,
+1.05 GJ at p = 0, 2.26 GJ at p = 1, with effective sample 2.46, 1.51 and 1.53 million rows.
+
+With the committed transmission loss of 138.7 dB at 3,730 km (sd 7.9 dB), the predicted received energy
+at H08S relative to the median airgun shot runs −37.4 to −4.1 dB across the three values of p and four
+decades of coupling efficiency:
+
+| coupling efficiency | predicted SNR, dB (p = 0 / 0.5 / 1) | P_D | likelihood ratio, non-detection |
+|---|---|---|---|
+| 1e-4 | −37.4 / −36.7 / −34.1 | 0.007 | 1.000 |
+| 1e-3 | −27.4 / −26.7 / −24.1 | 0.007 | 1.000 |
+| 1e-2 | −17.4 / −16.7 / −14.1 | 0.007 to 0.011 | 1.000 to 0.996 |
+| 1e-1 | −7.4 / −6.7 / −4.1 | 0.129 to 0.382 | 0.877 to 0.622 |
+
+**At every coupling efficiency up to 1e-2, at all three values of the dive probability, the detection
+probability is at the detector's own false-alarm rate and the likelihood ratio is 1.000.** The
+non-detection carries no information. Only at 1e-1 — three decades above the F-35A anchor — does it
+begin to, and weakly.
+
+Two corrections were made to this construction before any value was quoted. The likelihood ratio
+initially divided a detection probability measured under the ±3 robust-z outlier rule by the 0.3
+p-value screen level, which are two different detectors; that gave a ratio above 1 for a
+non-detection, which cannot be right. Both sides now use the same detector, with its own false-alarm
+rate of 0.0067 taken as the floor of its own injection curve. Separately, two of the four Cape Leeuwin
+statistics were indexed from their window start without their own base lag, putting the RMS window 2.0 s
+early and the matched filter 2.0 s late; centring is now derived from each series' window length and
+lag. The A1 verdict is unchanged by that fix.
+
+At coupling 1e-4 and 1e-3 the predicted level is **below the lowest injected level** of the committed
+curve, so P_D there is an extrapolation to the floor and is flagged as such in `likelihood_ratio.csv`.
+
+**Perth Canyon has no likelihood ratio.** There is no committed transmission loss at 1,422 km on that
+path, and building one is Task 2. Rather than substitute a spreading bracket, the measured p-values,
+SNR and maximum level are reported and the predicted level is recorded as not assessed.

+- **Feasible set.** The conditional asserts a source at the Kadri bearing's arc crossing, 26.2 S
+  101.9 E. The impact *energy* and duration come from `run-i-dive` marginally, because the conditional
+  fixes location and not energy.
+- **Model's reach.** Receivers: three of seven carry information for a source this far north; four are

+  train exists, which is H08S only; the caption window at H08S supports four of the six cycle
+  statistics and not the two that need a closed cycle.

+  sample 1.5 to 2.5 million. Perth Canyon 3376 covers 26.0 s of each 26 s window; 3315 covers none.
+- **Gaps.** (i) No transmission loss on the 26.2 S to Perth Canyon path — blocking for the Perth Canyon
+  likelihood ratio, Task 2. (ii) P_D below the lowest injected level at coupling 1e-4 and 1e-3 —

+  caption option's H08S window cannot be tested on the two closed-cycle statistics at all — permanent
+  on these data; the raw record is Kadri's.
+- **ESS per value of p:** 2.46e6 at 0.5, 1.51e6 at 0, 1.53e6 at 1.

+## 7. Caveat wording, verbatim
+
+> Peak radiated mechanical power is not an acoustic source level; the coupling is a declared
+> alternative with a prior, not a constant.
+
+> The propagation model passes a level check against the 2001 airguns and fails a frequency-slope
+> check by +10 dB per octave at Cape Leeuwin and +7 at Diego Garcia South; the airgun data cannot
+> separate the source spectrum, the array pattern and the propagation.
+
+> Detection probability is measured in IMOS logger noise and shifted by +4.8 dB to stand in for a
+> three-element IMS station. It is a proxy.
+
+> In the predicted windows no transient exceeded the pre-registered false-alarm level; the smallest
+> resolvable p is 0.0101. That is no significant detection with limited power, not an absence.
+
+> Module likelihood remains zero until injection-recovery gives detection probability against
+> received level.
