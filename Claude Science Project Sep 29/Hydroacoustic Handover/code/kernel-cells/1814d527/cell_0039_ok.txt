+## A. Derived products in the request windows **[Kadri]**
+
+**Why:** the module's windows are now built on the accepted impact set (run J) and cover every data treatment of the 00:19
+log-on, so a single pass over these windows settles what H01W and H08S hold for every hypothesis family at once. Our own
+tests ran on the digitised Fig. 9 panels (plots, ~7 Hz envelope resolution, 30 + 20 min), which cover 54 % (H01W) and
+69 % (H08S) of the predicted arrival mass under the primary option and nothing of the late half of the H01W window.
+
+Windows (from `windows.csv`; union over the four data options, −10/+20 min padding):

+| receiver | SOFAR | AGW | 99 % interval, primary option (00:19 held out, p = 0.5) |
+|---|---|---|---|
+| H01W | 00:25–01:35 | 00:25–01:40 | 00:39:23–01:14:23 |
+| H08S | 00:50–01:55 | 00:50–01:55 | 01:01:13–01:32:23 |
+| H08N (blocked for arc impacts in our model; low priority) | 00:50–01:55 | 00:50–02:00 | 01:03:33–01:34:39 |

+For H01W and H08S, both branches:
+1. Band-level time series at 1 s resolution for 2–5, 5–10, 10–20, 20–40 and 5–40 Hz (his chain: 5 Hz high-pass, 2–40 Hz
+   band-pass), per element and element mean, in dB re 1 µPa, instrument response removed; and the raw 2–5 Hz series
+   without the 5 Hz high-pass, because that filter removes the AGW band.
+2. The outputs of his detector (windowed entropy) unchanged, with its threshold, over the same windows.
+3. Triad bearings and SNR for every detection, with the method's error (he reports 3.3°; we test with a t₃ at that scale).
+4. The same products for the **same-night controls**: the request windows shifted −6 h and +6 h (H01W 18:25–19:40 on
+   7 March and 06:25–07:40 on 8 March; H08S 18:50–19:55 and 06:50–07:55), and for the **same UTC windows on 1–14 March
+   2014** (13 nights excluding 8 March). These set the false-alarm rate of every statistic we will quote.
+5. Optional, if cheap: the same for 00:00–02:00 on 8 March as a continuous strip (his Fig. 9 panels cover 00:27–00:57 and
+   01:00–01:20; the module's H01W window runs to 01:14 under the primary option and the controls need the continuity).

+## B. Injection-recovery with his detector unchanged **[Kadri]**
+
+**Why:** the module's detection-probability model for the IMS stations is borrowed from an IMOS logger with a +4.8 dB
+triad gain; this is the gate that decides whether hydroacoustics can enter the likelihood at all (block 7). We ask him to
+inject our pulse model (`pulse_runj.npz`: a KRAKEN deep-channel arrival at 2,200 km with the run J source duration, 5–40 Hz;
+and the H08S-range template from `templates_envelope.npz`) into real H01W and H08S background from the control windows at
+the received 5–40 Hz 1 s SPL values below, 100+ trials per level, and report the fraction his detector (unchanged) and the
+M1b statistic recover, with the false-alarm rate at the same threshold.

+## D. Ground-truth recordings or derived products **[either]**
+
+**Why:** the coupling efficiency (C2) rests on one event and the propagation slope (C1) on one 2003 airgun line. Any of
+these would move a caveat.
+1. **F-35A, 2019** (Metz, Obana & Fukao 2023; the paper is closed access to us — gap G4): the H11 (Wake) waveform or its
+   band-level series and spectrum in 2–60 Hz, the published 0.7 Pa peak and the window used. If he holds the IMS record or
+   the derived products, that is our second coupling anchor; if not, an introduction to Metz's group.
+2. **ARA San Juan, 15 Nov 2017**: H10N and H04S waveforms or band levels of the implosion arrival (his own 2019 paper is
+   closed to us — gap G5); this is the calibration of the deep-channel slope on an independent path.
+3. **Blackman et al. (2004) shots**, if his archive reaches 2001 and 2003: the air7 and air9 lines at H01W and H08S (air7
+   is weak at Cape Leeuwin and the plotted TL may be a noise-selected ceiling) and any SUS shot with a received level in
+   stated units (our level test of the EW0112 source model failed on a units mismatch — gap G23; a matched-units received
+   level is what closes it).

+## E. Specific questions **[Kadri]**
+
+1. **Which signal carries the 306.18° bearing?** The 2024 text gives 00:52:03 and the Fig. 9 caption 00:54:30 for the
+   signal of interest; Table 1 lists 306.18° once. Does the bearing belong to the text time, the caption time, or both
+   (two signals)? On our run J windows both times fall inside every option's 99 % interval; our envelope matched filter marks
+   00:52:04 on the digitised panel (c) at p = 0.043 and the caption-time box at p = 0.12. The 306.18° bearing is
+   uninformative on run J (the bearing line crosses the arc near 25.7°S, where 0.5–1 % of our mass lies).
+2. **What do the four panel-(a) boxes on the SnT 2025 poster denote?** (about 00:28:12–00:29:42, 00:30:24–00:31:54,
+   00:32:30–00:34:00, 00:35:24–00:36:54, labelled "weak"). The arc's closest approach to H01W is about 1,563 km (17.6 min at
+   1.482 km/s), so boxes 1–3 end before the earliest deep-channel arrival from any arc impact after 00:19:29 (00:36:51) and
+   box 4 is marginal. Are they candidate arrivals, calibration marks, or something else; and do they have bearings?
+3. **Table 1 rows 00:49:58 (260.4°) and 00:53:31 (257.6°)**: on run J these two rows are consistent in both time and bearing
+   with the core impact region (implied impact times 00:26:47 and 00:29:13, inside the 50 % band; log₁₀ Bayes factor 1.2 and
+   1.5 against the arc-uniform alternative). Neither is marked in Fig. 9. Do they have triad bearings with SNR, and what did
+   his detector make of them?
+4. **H08S 01:05–01:20**: our lag-consistent candidate for the H01W 00:52:04 row sits at 01:12:49 (raw panel, typical of the
+   shot-dominated record) and the suppressed-residual row at 01:07:52. What does the H08S record hold between 01:05 and
+   01:20 after the shots are removed, with bearings?
+5. **Time base**: are the Fig. 9 and Table 1 times UTC with the GPS leap-second offset applied (16 s in 2014)? Royer's 2016
+   report shows an internal 7.7–16.6 s offset between his H01W table picks and his figure marks; we need to know which base
+   each of the two archives uses before we compare them at the second level.
