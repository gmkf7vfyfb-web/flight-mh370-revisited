## E. Specific questions **[Kadri]**

1. **Which signal carries the 306.18° bearing?** The 2024 text gives 00:52:03 and the Fig. 9 caption 00:54:30 for the
   signal of interest; Table 1 lists 306.18° once. Does the bearing belong to the text time, the caption time, or both
   (two signals)? On our run J windows both times fall inside every option's 99 % interval; our envelope matched filter marks
   00:52:04 on the digitised panel (c) at p = 0.043 and the caption-time box at p = 0.12. The 306.18° bearing is
   uninformative on run J (the bearing line crosses the arc near 25.7°S, where 0.5–1 % of our mass lies).
2. **What do the four panel-(a) boxes on the SnT 2025 poster denote?** (about 00:28:12–00:29:42, 00:30:24–00:31:54,
   00:32:30–00:34:00, 00:35:24–00:36:54, labelled "weak"). The arc's closest approach to H01W is about 1,563 km (17.6 min at
   1.482 km/s), so boxes 1–3 end before the earliest deep-channel arrival from any arc impact after 00:19:29 (00:36:51) and
   box 4 is marginal. Are they candidate arrivals, calibration marks, or something else; and do they have bearings?
3. **Table 1 rows 00:49:58 (260.4°) and 00:53:31 (257.6°)**: on run J these two rows are consistent in both time and bearing
   with the core impact region (implied impact times 00:26:47 and 00:29:13, inside the 50 % band; log₁₀ Bayes factor 1.2 and
   1.5 against the arc-uniform alternative). Neither is marked in Fig. 9. Do they have triad bearings with SNR, and what did
   his detector make of them?
4. **H08S 01:05–01:20**: our lag-consistent candidate for the H01W 00:52:04 row sits at 01:12:49 (raw panel, typical of the
   shot-dominated record) and the suppressed-residual row at 01:07:52. What does the H08S record hold between 01:05 and
   01:20 after the shots are removed, with bearings?
5. **Time base**: are the Fig. 9 and Table 1 times UTC with the GPS leap-second offset applied (16 s in 2014)? Royer's 2016
   report shows an internal 7.7–16.6 s offset between his H01W table picks and his figure marks; we need to know which base
   each of the two archives uses before we compare them at the second level.
