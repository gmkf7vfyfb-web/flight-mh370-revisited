Every number in predictions.csv and windows.csv is PREDICTIVE, NOT CALIBRATED. The module's likelihood is 0.0 in every row
and stays so until injection-recovery on real IMS background gives a detection probability (the block-7 gate, pending).
Two caveats travel with every number:
  C1  Propagation slope misfit. Against the 2003 Blackman airgun line (air9) the module's transmission-loss chain has the
      level about right (median residual -0.2 dB at H01W, +3.2 dB at H08S) but a frequency slope of +10.4 +- 0.9 dB per
      octave (H01W) and +7.4 +- 0.7 (H08S) that it does not reproduce. Tonight's recalibration (block 9) shows that an
      EW0112-derived airgun source spectrum removes most of that tilt (common mode from about 9 to -1.2 +- 1.8 dB/oct; the
      same source also removes the tilt on an independent path, air1 -> H08N, +0.8 +- 1.8 dB/oct), which attributes the
      slope to the airgun source rather than to the path; but the pre-registered level test failed for a units reason
      (peak level vs spectrum level, new gap G23) and the rule required it, so C1 is UNCHANGED. Band-to-band TL shape
      in this table may be wrong by that order; the 5-40 Hz band total is less affected than any single band.
  C2  Coupling efficiency eta (share of impact kinetic energy that becomes sound) is NOT calibrated. One event anchors it:
      the F-35A crash (eta about 2e-4 from the published 0.7 Pa at 3,300 km and 900 +- 200 MJ; the module's RAM-corrected
      median is 2.1e-4, used as the "upper anchor"). AF447 and Yemenia give upper limits only. The table therefore sweeps
      eta over {1e-2, 1e-3, 2.1e-4, 1e-4, 1e-5}; the eta that would make a cell detectable (SNR 0 dB) is given per row.
