Calibration file names are written in the Metadata text file.

Calibrations were carried out by:
inputting White noise of known level into the system with the hydrophone in series.
White noise level was -90 dB re 1 V^2/Hz
The low frequency roll off was deliberately applied to flatten the sea noise 
The impedance match of hydrophone and pre-amplifier is correctly accounted for in the calibration
The low frequency roll off will differ with different hydrophones (different capacitance)

The instrument settings during calibration were identical to those used in the field
Calibrations were carried out pre- and post- deployment. The file supplied is pre-deployment.

A power spectra of the calibration file will give the system gain with frequency (difference between measured and -90 input)

The system gain can be used with the hydrophone sensitivity to convert volts to Pa.
