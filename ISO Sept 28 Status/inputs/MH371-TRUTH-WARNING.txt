inputs/acars/ holds the ACARS workbook, which contains the MH371 truth track.
The MH371 known-flight control is blind: only its scorer (report/mh371_score.py) may read that
file. No agent building, configuring or tuning the estimator may open it or any copy of it
(this folder, mh371-acars.xlsx at the repository root, or the Hugging Face dissertation
spreadsheets).
