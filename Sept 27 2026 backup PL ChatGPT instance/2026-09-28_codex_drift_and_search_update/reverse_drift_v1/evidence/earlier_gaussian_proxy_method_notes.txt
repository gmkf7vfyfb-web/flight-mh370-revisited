Pleiades-conditioned integrated impact posterior — working analysis

Pleiades likelihood
- Conditional hypothesis Himg=1: at least some objects in the 23 March Pleiades images are MH370 debris.
- Source centers from CSIRO Part III: east1 35.6 S, 92.8 E; west1 alternatives 34.7 S, 92.6 E and
  35.3 S, 91.8 E.
- Baseline mixture weights 0.50/0.25/0.25 are scenario weights, NOT probabilities published by CSIRO.
- Gaussian spatial widths: 25 km along the local 7th-arc tangent and 10 km cross-arc. These translate
  CSIRO's statements that locations within about 50 km essentially parallel to the arc are also plausible
  and that it did not have enough confidence to determine the optimum width of the 10–30 km strips.
- A publication version should reproduce the BRAN2015/BRAN2016 drift simulations directly rather than
  treating these three source regions as Gaussian components.

EOF kernels
- No displacement: diagnostic only.
- DST historical 15/30 NM: spatial density uniform inside 15 NM, with Gaussian falloff of 30 NM SD outside.
- 15/50 and 15/100 NM use the same mathematical form with broader tails. These are sensitivity envelopes,
  not claims that Davey/ATSB endorsed those values.

This analysis therefore answers the counterfactual:
'If at least some Pleiades objects were MH370 debris, how does that evidence reweight the integrated
SATCOM/RF airborne posterior under alternative EOF-distance assumptions?'
