## Bayesian estimator: diagnosis and candidate implementations

The active cruise route uses a forward bootstrap filter and global-ESS-triggered resampling within fixed strata. It has no active backward simulation or full-history rejuvenation. Command proposals include importance corrections, but narrow guidance based on an unreliable forecast can still have very large weight variance. The failed large synthetic controls are already highly concentrated at 19:41, before the final fuel factor; late fuel conditioning is therefore not a sufficient explanation on its own.

Across the six saved cruise populations, weighted starting-root ESS is 20.95–152.41. The combined 1,020,000-row pool has root ESS 356.91 and current-row weight ESS 6031.34. The separate full-model 1000-candidate diagnostic, repeated with one and two threads, produces byte-identical scientific outputs; root ESS falls from 161.52 at 18:28 to 37.86 at 19:41 and 3.226 at the final contact. One root carries 47.43% of the final mass. Later observations can rebalance surviving roots, so root ESS need not decline monotonically; roots with no descendants cannot reappear without a history-changing move.

Root ESS, computed from total weight per initial root, quantifies weighted ancestry concentration. It is not a general effective number of independent trajectories or a posterior-accuracy measure. Independent-seed agreement and known-truth recovery remain necessary. The current maps' fine-scale failure to replicate supplies an additional, distinct warning. Across all 15 pairs of the six cruise runs, maximum weighted latitude-CDF differences range from 6.88 to 19.50 percentage points and longitude differences from 9.01 to 20.94 points. The declared scientific target is common, with corrected computational proposal differences. Individual north-of-35°S masses are 5.39–10.66%; the evidence-weighted pool gives 8.76%, and leaving one run out gives 8.25–9.18%. These comparisons are descriptive numerical disagreements, not KS tests or calibrated uncertainty bounds. The leave-one-out northern summary is more stable than the full marginal shapes.

Two compiled Rust edge cases have separate patches: normalization of equal log weights near −1e20 incorrectly gives probabilities [1,1], and a zero systematic-resampling offset can select an initial zero-mass slot. Focused regressions and the corrected 79-test particle-filter suite pass. Neither defect has been shown to cause the main MH370 concentration at ordinary numerical scales. The full code audit also distinguishes defects in unused experimental helpers from the active computation.

A new whole-history SMC implementation was tested on analytic, adversarial and restricted physical targets. Its initial missed-mode failure and a normalization defect found in the review's own first candidate are retained with their corrections. On a two-leg physical SATCOM target with actual observations through 19:41, independent whole-support quadrature and the reference differ by 5.23e-9 in log evidence. This is numerical agreement between calculations, not a rigorous error bound. Over eight seeds and equal likelihood-evaluation counts, SMC reduces mean maximum marginal-CDF error from 6.89 to 4.34 percentage points against **prior-trajectory importance sampling**. It improves six seeds, is about 1.5 times slower, and has worse normalizer error. This comparator is not the original production sequential filter. The target fixes important flight variables and omits wind and fuel; it is a numerical development control, not a new MH370 posterior.

The defensible implementation direction is to retain stable weight arithmetic, use exact proposal-density corrections, and add moves that actually revise early states and controls while replaying their downstream likelihood. A larger population of descendants alone cannot provide that capability. The supplied restricted implementation is reusable evidence for development; successful full-flight integration and calibration remain open.

## Numerical and scientific sensitivities that matter

**Fuel.** Nine controls with exact compatibility probability 0.0571317791 receive nine-node estimates 0.0477238614–0.0659645612. The boundary-aware adaptive candidate agrees with independent integration and the closed form at about 1e-15. A 21-state comparison demonstrates improvement as fixed quadrature is refined. The historical Ulich/OpenAP comparison also contains shifted engine-table columns, including a NOx value used as takeoff fuel flow. That historical bug is distinct from the canonical Martin-based model. Correcting it changes the benchmark without validating a calibrated replacement.

**Terminal dynamics.** The proposed midpoint-position patch restores second-order behavior in the specifically tested smooth meridional spatial-gradient controls. It leaves a separate non-meridional geodesic-stepping inconsistency unchanged. All 723 archived near-vertical exits are upward, and all 2530 minimum-speed exits are climbing. Among 45,875 computed-impact rows, 1765 exceed Mach 1 under the declared lower-clamped atmosphere; these are unweighted proposal-row counts. A fixed public drag polar in that regime is an extrapolation. No posterior probability or observed accident state is inferred from these counts. A constructed event-order failure is also retained; no accepted archived impact violates the saved 50/700 m/s cutoffs.

**Evidence attribution.** On the same saved impact populations, north-of-35°S mass is 2.33–4.00% before updates and 2.33–4.01% after native drift-domain restriction. After that restriction it is 19.64–32.51% with search but without recovery, 29.22–40.39% with recovery but without search, and 73.96–83.25% with both. These ranges are model/repeat comparisons, not confidence intervals. They show why the separate airborne 8.8% figure must not be treated as the same population before a single update.

**Search geometry and detection.** Replacing the simplified mask with the complete source changes membership at 82 of 45,517 saved coordinates. Principal north probabilities change by −0.39949 to +0.04928 percentage points; the maximum principal total variation is 0.0044584. This does not explain the main northward redistribution. Direct inspection of ATSB Figure 73 now verifies its combined area shares of 97.4% high confidence, 2.1% lower confidence and 0.5% gaps. Substituting category detection rates of 95%, 70% and zero gives exactly 94% for those rounded shares. The source actually states greater than 95% for the first class. The calculation explains the approximate reference; it does not justify a constant pointwise detection rate or weighting by the aircraft posterior. Retaining detailed holes while also applying an aggregate gap discount requires explicit modelling review. New reported survey totals cannot supply missing spatial quality information.

**Display precision.** Holding the scientific weights fixed, four 95% display areas range around 114,000–131,000 km² at 10 km smoothing, 200,000–220,000 km² at 25 km, and 361,000–379,000 km² at 50 km. Unsmoothed areas are much more grid-dependent. Neither compact raw cells nor broad smoothed contours establish calibrated uncertainty. Independent frozen 7500 km² cell evaluations remain far below their design-run mass, even after the geometry correction.

**Pléiades.** Family total variations 0.484–0.570 and mode differences 54–94 km reproduce. A separate daily-wind knot shift changes shapes by total variation 0.051–0.077 and moves OSCAR's mode 29.2 km. It is a declared sensitivity, not proof of a uniquely correct interpolation convention. Fixed equal-family weights, source support, image association and arithmetic object averaging must remain explicit. GA-derived pixel scales make agreement with GA area partly a construction check. Six inherited segmentation dimension records need correction; available added-family decisions are unchanged.

**Hydroacoustics.** Independently recovered PDF plot-axis correction gains are approximately 1.0971, 1.5000 and 1.1250 for three panels. These correct publication-figure digitization, not hydrophone instrument calibration. Absolute amplitude and squared-pressure quantities change, but affine centering/scaling cancels in the normalized test pipeline. The complete corrected run has identical adjusted probabilities. No tested association passes both background models.

**Ocean inference.** Selected later cowling controls improve, yet the two complete cowling surfaces still differ and the remaining eight recovery factors are shared. Modal-cell weighted root ESS is about 32 and 18, despite 4096 nominal paths. A spatial kernel combined with maximization over delay and an imperfect coastline/support mask is not a calibrated recovery observation process merely because its source surface is normalized. Shared environmental errors and unknown recovery effort remain consequential.
