Changes from the first integrated run
1. Radar initialisation moved from a loose 18:22 prior to the final regular radar epoch at 18:01:49.
   Davey specifies 0.5 NM position uncertainty and 1 degree heading uncertainty and states that the 18:22
   point was not used numerically, but was used as evidence that the aircraft did not turn between 18:02
   and 18:22. The precise numerical 18:01:49 mean coordinate is not tabulated in the source text, so the
   current implementation uses a reconstructed mean (5.624829 N, 99.048157 E, track 295.66 deg) and
   explicitly stress-tests the mean by about +/-0.8 NM.
2. The BFO residual is decomposed into fast measurement noise plus a correlated latent bias:
       sigma_fast = 1.5 Hz
       total known-flight residual scale = 3.4 Hz
       implied stationary slow-bias sigma = sqrt(3.4^2 - 1.5^2) = 3.051 Hz
       primary slow-bias correlation time = 1 hour.
   The 18:25 BFO remains excluded.
3. The gain-survival/precompensation coefficient remains marginalised using the independent MH371 /
   known-MH370 calibration.
4. Exact ACCESS-G March 2014 numerical fields are still not available locally. The primary model therefore
   retains the Davey OU wind-error model around zero nominal wind. Wind uncertainty stress tests at 0x and
   2x its standard scale were performed; these are robustness tests, not substitutes for ACCESS-G.
