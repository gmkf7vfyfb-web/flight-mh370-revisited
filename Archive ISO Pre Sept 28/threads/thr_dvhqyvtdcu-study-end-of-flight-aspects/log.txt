      ## Interpretation limits

      The amber surface is a display KDE over 32 impact-conditioned particles
      (effective sample size 28.71) from one explicitly selected low-flow,
      equal-feed point-mass scenario. The bivariate Gaussian plotting kernel uses
      the weighted spatial covariance scaled by `ESS^(-1/6)`, with a small numerical
      regularizer. Its 50%, 90%, and 95% contours describe that smoothed display
      surface; the smoothing choice is not another evidence likelihood.

      The magenta Pleiades/BRAN2016 grid is an independently derived conditional
      surface. It is normalized and rendered separately, solely for visual
      comparison. The two surfaces are not combined and their colors do not encode
      relative model probability.

      The handoff explicitly reports aircraft attitude and contact duration as
      unresolved (`null`). The ten perspective panels therefore align a simple
      aircraft glyph to the resolved impact velocity and impose zero roll and zero
      sideslip for display only. They must not be interpreted as sampled body
      attitudes or first-contact configurations.
