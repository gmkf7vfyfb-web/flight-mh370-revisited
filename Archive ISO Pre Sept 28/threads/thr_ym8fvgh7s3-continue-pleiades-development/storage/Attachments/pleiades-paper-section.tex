\section{Pl\'eiades imagery: morphology and conditional source-location analysis}
\label{sec:pleiades-conditional}

On 23 March 2014 near 04:00 UTC, Pl\'eiades 1A acquired four 0.5~m
southern Indian Ocean scenes. Supplied by French military intelligence and
CNES, the imagery was examined by Geoscience Australia at the ATSB's
request~\citep{minchin2017pleiades}. Twelve of at least 70 catalogued objects
were rated 5 (probably not natural objects or features). This did not identify
aircraft debris. Targets 06, 18, 19, 26 and 27 were the five rating-5 masks
stable through 18 segmentation perturbations; the others had weaker geometry.
Selection measured segmentation stability, not aircraft resemblance.

Eight equal-sized families tested flooding-model and random-pose wings; other
777 parts; matched random geometry; ocean controls; the whole fuselage; and
documented-boundary or random-cut fuselage sections. Boeing geometry
was used~\citep{boeing2024acap}; controls represented containers, nets/ropes,
clustered cargo/plastic/timber, wreckage/hulls and fish-aggregating
devices~\citep{hapag2016container,iotc2023observer,lebreton2018plastic}.
\citet{murphy2013structures} gives six 777 section intervals, including BS~2150
at the aft pressure bulkhead. These reproducible boundaries are not asserted
weak points. Contrasting accidents include Asiana separation there, a local
777 puncture without barrel breakup, and A320 ditching damage to aft skin,
bulkhead and tail cone~\citep{aaib2010ba38,ntsb2010hudson,ntsb2014asiana}.
They do not define an MH370 breakup prior.

Each family contained 3,372 masks: 26,976 masks and 134,880 family--object
comparisons. Fuselage orientation was isotropic; one fifth were fully visible,
otherwise a linear waterline targeted 15--100\% visibility. These are
sampling-coverage sensitivities, not pose or flooding probabilities. Ranking
required visible area, oriented length and width all inside the target
intervals. Principal-axis-normalised masks were compared under identity and
three reflections. For eligible candidate $c$, target $o$, family $g$, masks
$T_o,P_o$, reflection set $\mathcal F$, and size-eligible set $E_o$,
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{IoU}(A,B)
    &=\frac{|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|},\\
  \ell(c,o)
    &=1-\frac{
      \max_{f\in\mathcal F}\operatorname{IoU}(T_o,f(c))+
      \max_{f\in\mathcal F}\operatorname{IoU}(P_o,f(c))
    }{2},\\
  \ell^{*}_{g,o}
    &=\min_{c\in g\cap E_o}\ell(c,o).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:pleiades-mask-loss}
\end{equation}
Lower loss is greater overlap, not an identity probability.

Extended minima were nacelle-side (06), matched random geometry (18/19),
random-pose wing (26), and documented section 47 (27). The last loss was
0.3058 versus 0.3145 for random geometry, but 0.3556 when only fully visible
section masks were admitted. The whole shell ranked lowest for none. Its
eligible minima required axis/view cosines 0.885--0.988 and visible fractions
0.305--0.672: near-end-on projection, occlusion, or both. Random geometry
matched pooled dimension ranges, not outlines, and was deliberately
competitive. 777 shapes were admissible but not preferentially selected;
morphology remained non-specific.

For conditional source location, \citet{griffin2017drift} propagated debris
forward from trial sites under the assumption that some objects
were from 9M-MRO. A separate clean-sheet forward-ensemble reconstruction
released particles from 5,289 cells along and within $\pm100$~NM of the
seventh arc. BRAN2016 and OSCAR v2 currents were evaluated
separately~\citep{dohan2021oscar}, with common NCEP
winds~\citep{kalnay1996reanalysis}, three windage alternatives and
5~NM/day dispersion. All twelve location likelihoods were averaged equally;
no object was randomly drawn. The five-object set was a sensitivity.

BRAN's mode was 35.418$^{\circ}$S, 92.886$^{\circ}$E with a 24,309~km$^2$ 90\%
region; OSCAR's was 34.917$^{\circ}$S, 92.047$^{\circ}$E with 18,393~km$^2$.
Averaging uniform subsets of one to five objects reproduces that PDF for any
subset-size prior; a product needs correlated debris and detection models. The
50:50 BRAN--OSCAR prior mixture has mode 35.300$^{\circ}$S,
92.891$^{\circ}$E and a 25,896~km$^2$ 90\% region. It is not
independent-evidence multiplication or a calibrated posterior. These are
conditional hypotheses for an integrated estimator; none establishes identity
or updates the main estimate unconditionally.
