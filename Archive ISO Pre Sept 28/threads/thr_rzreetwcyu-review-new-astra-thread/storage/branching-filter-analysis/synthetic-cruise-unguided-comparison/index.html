Every route and noisy contact sequence was generated before inference began. The estimator receives the generated measurements and original environment, with no access to its generating route. Generating routes were accepted by an independent uniform draw against the same remaining-fuel-envelope probability used in inference. Acceptance uses no SATCOM measurement or noise value. This checks computation conditional on that envelope, not the physical validity of the fuel model or an exact exhaustion trajectory. The accident-specific initial proposal is disabled for this control. Commands and event times use their original prior.
