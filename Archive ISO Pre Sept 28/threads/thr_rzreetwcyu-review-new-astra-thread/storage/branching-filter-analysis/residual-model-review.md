# Searched-area residuals, final-contact uncertainty and estimator stability

Reviewed 2026-09-15T10:44:32.076662+00:00. This is a code and saved-result audit, with a proposed implementation order. No new aircraft/ocean simulation, combined hypothesis PDF or scientific default change was performed. Exact numerical inputs and source hashes are in `residual-model-review.json` alongside this note.

## What is already integrated

The canonical search-evidence spoke applies the likelihood of no detection to particle weights and returns both normalized residual weights and the normalization constant. Interior polygon holes are unassessed; within-campaign overlaps are united. Shared misses and conditionally independent misses across campaigns are explicit alternatives. These are calculated residual PDFs, conditional on declared coverage and detection assumptions.

The ATSB L0 footprint is a geographic approximation, not a pointwise usable-sonar quality mask. Detection values 50%, 80% and 94% are sensitivity settings. The 94% reference is our calculation 0.974 × 0.95 + 0.021 × 0.70 from ATSB (2017), Figure 73, printed p.96 / PDF p.105; the figure actually describes high-confidence coverage as >95%. It is not a measured 94% probability at every point. Known polygon holes and the report's aggregate gap discount do not provide an exact matching coverage accounting. The optional 2018 outline is approximate and its 60% effective detection is also a declared assumption.

The 8 March 2026 MOT update reports approximately 7,571 km² surveyed in the 2025/26 campaign, but supplies no usable swath geometry or detection-quality mask. The local evidence register therefore does not spatially apply this campaign. Vessel tracks and planned areas cannot substitute for bottom coverage. Existing residuals should retain coverage/detection sensitivities until authoritative spatial information improves them. A normalized residual PDF integrates to one; its pre-normalization constant is the probability that the specified searches would miss under that input model, not a separate probability that the aircraft remains missing.

## How to combine the 00:19 alternatives

Use four joint BFO reliability states: neither usable, R600 only, R1200 only, both. Preserve common accepted corrected BTO and contact-time information. The existing whole-transmission 'neither' case also omits BTO and airborne survival constraints, so it is not interchangeable with 'neither BFO'. Stored BTO-retaining variants already cover the four BFO selections, but timing/survival rules must also be matched explicitly. Do not silently equate conditioning on one used contact with survival through both contacts.

A declared mixture of conditional PDFs is possible immediately from stored particles after matching those conditions. Its weights are assumptions to vary and report. Equal weights are one sensitivity choice, not evidence that all states are equally likely. For data-inferred model probabilities, every reliability state must describe the same observations using a normalized model for unreliable measurements. Raw normalizers from existing cases that drop different observations cannot be compared as model probabilities. An electronics warm-up model should be a linked instrument-error alternative with uncertain parameters and prior weight, not an extra equally weighted vote. The present uniform warm-up offsets are not calibrated to this short interruption.

For pre-search component weights a_m, normalized component densities p_m(x), and common miss likelihood L(x):

    q(x) = sum_m a_m p_m(x)
    p_residual(x) = L(x) q(x) / integral L(u) q(u) du
    Z_m = integral L(x) p_m(x) dx
    a_m_after_search = a_m Z_m / sum_k a_k Z_k

Thus the search also changes the relative probabilities of the final-contact alternatives. Combining separately normalized residual maps with unchanged pre-search model weights would miss that update. Drift can likewise inform component probabilities when its observation model is comparable across components. Do not multiply any evidence twice. Keep physical model families explicit; a model is not scientifically preferable because its sampler behaves better. Report uncomputed impacts and unsupported drift locations separately rather than quietly treating them as physically excluded or absorbing their missing mass into model odds.

One fuel-conditioned synthetic stress case was already run twice at 500,000 initial candidates per run. Its independent latitude/longitude CDF differences were 64.8/63.2 percentage points and one ancestral family carried 95.6%/98.2% of final weight. Its truth ended at 18.08°N, 91.12°E; it is not the MH370 southern endpoint case. This shows that a large nominal sample does not establish general recovery, but does not by itself refute the actual southern pattern.

The recorded stress-case ancestry collapses after the first long gap between satellite updates, before the final fuel condition. Tight satellite likelihoods select a few histories; branching/resampling makes descendants without restoring discarded histories. The fuel compatibility term at the final 00:11 update can add concentration. Terminal guidance has a separate demonstrated failure to cover the control histories favored by paired BFOs. Recent guide variants helped some cases and worsened others, and were not adopted. Broad conclusions can be useful while precise hotspot ranking remains unresolved. Genuine sensitivity to different physical assumptions must be distinguished from repeat variability under unchanged assumptions.

## Implementation order

1. Prepare matched before-search and residual maps from existing impact/debris populations, retaining ATSB detection and optional 2018 sensitivities. Clearly identify recent unassessed coverage. Preserve 50/90/99% contours, input conditions and evidence normalizers.
2. Add an explicitly weighted four-state final-BFO mixture with common BTO/timing conditions, report weight sensitivity, and apply unsuccessful searches to the mixture before final normalization. Retain constituent maps. A calibrated joint reliability model requires separate justification of bad-measurement and warm-up distributions.
3. Diagnose independent ancestry and regional repeat variability before and after every major update. Judge stability at the resolution of the claim: broad latitude ranges first; then fixed prospective search cells evaluated on independent samples. Do not reinstate an arbitrary 5% pass/fail threshold.
4. Correct proposals where saved diagnostics show support collapse: use candidate-specific observation predictions, preserve full physical support and exact importance corrections, and allow earlier histories to be revised. Test the correction on independent reference/synthetic cases before a targeted larger ensemble. Existing failed forward/backward or guide variants must not simply be rerun unchanged.

Steps 1–2 primarily reuse saved samples; previous comparable reweighting and figure jobs took minutes of computation. This is not a completion estimate for instrument calibration or estimator repair. No new large ensemble or uniquely optimal 7,500 km² search recommendation follows from this review alone. Current fuel support remains 00:15–00:19 UTC; the previously discussed 00:17:30-centred sensitivity has not been silently substituted.
