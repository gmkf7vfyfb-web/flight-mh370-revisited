## Weighted independent trajectory trees

Source: Davey, Gordon, Holland, Rutten and Williams (2016), *Bayesian Methods in the Search for MH370*, chapter 8, equations 8.4–8.6, Springer DOI 10.1007/978-981-10-0379-0_8. Local book SHA256: 37554ca5f0c0ef14fddb825748d9f9dc9059a26563f783a3e4dfafcd0ced91b2. Located chapter 8 text and equations were checked against publisher HTML; the local PDF is the 2016 published version.

Each starting root is drawn independently, conditional on its declared navigation stratum. If n_j roots represent prior mode probability p_j out of N roots, its initial weight is p_j N/n_j. Each observation multiplies the unnormalised weight by the observation likelihood and any fixed computational unit conversion. Above threshold eta, b descendants each receive w/b. Below eta, one descendant is retained with probability w/r, and receives weight r. Therefore the expected retained measure is w for every parent, and the roulette second moment is w*r. The literal published policy has r=1; the explicitly identified lower-variance policy sets r=eta. This extends the general expected-offspring identity in equation 8.4; it is not a claim that the authors used that specific variance reduction.

Computational likelihood multipliers, threshold, offspring count and roulette weight affect Monte Carlo effort/variance. The multipliers are subtracted from final log weights before computing evidence. They do not change the intended physical target. Finite runs can still be extremely inaccurate: expectation preservation is not a convergence guarantee. Complete fixed sets of root trees are used, with no result released after a time/node/leaf bound abort. Roots with no terminal contribution remain in the evidence denominator. Descendants share earlier random histories and are not independent samples.

The first 5,000-root independent pair with literal roulette and eta=exp(-25) failed severely. Neither its location density nor its navigation-family weights is accepted. A same-type manoeuvre clock discrepancy was subsequently corrected before the new pilot family. Those older outputs are retained solely as diagnostics with their original executable hashes.

## Cruise model

The reference family uses the published five navigation modes, OU cruise perturbations, Mach 0.73–0.84, 25,000–43,000 ft in 1,000-ft increments, uniform turn extents within +/-180 degrees, fixed 15-degree bank, nominal Mach rate 0.1/minute and altitude rate 4,000 ft/minute. The shared mean delay between manoeuvres has a declared log-uniform 0.1–10 hour prior. The delay for a change of a given kind begins after the previous change of that kind completes (chapter 7 p.48). Different kinds may overlap. The finite-rate model is kinematic and does not prove thrust/stall feasibility at every state. The baseline follows the book's omission of vertical Doppler; this must be a separately labelled sensitivity for the physical extension.

Inputs substitute ERA5 and IGRF grids for the historical weather and magnetic datasets. Grid-domain rejection restricts support and must be quantified. Magnetic altitude is clamped to the supplied grid bounds. Initial navigation-family probabilities are equal by declaration. LNAV uses WGS84 geodesic azimuth transport and may switch once to true or magnetic heading. Radar control-angle interpretation, environmental and satellite calibration, and routine step-climb/speed-change priors remain model assumptions to audit. This is not an assumption-free probability density or an exact input-level recreation of the published work.

## Measurement-guided command proposals

A later bounded experiment adds an auxiliary proposal at turn, Mach and altitude events. It draws K candidate commands from the unchanged command prior, predicts each approximately to the upcoming contact with future commands omitted, and selects using a mixture of uniform and guide-likelihood probabilities q_j. Its exact auxiliary correction is 1/(K*q_j). Conditional expectation over selection recovers the ordinary candidate average, so even an inaccurate forecast preserves the intended unnormalised target. The positive uniform component preserves candidate support and bounds this one-event correction. Repeated corrections can still produce high variance; the physical observation is always evaluated on the full stochastic propagation, not the cheap forecast.

The cheap forecast is a deterministic, frozen-environment approximation with an analytic finite-turn displacement and a subsequent geodesic leg. Observation errors are inflated only inside the proposal guide; original BTO/BFO uncertainties remain in the physical likelihood. A deliberately inaccurate binary guide control recovers independently calculated joint masses, including the 1/K factor. This verifies auxiliary weighting, not full flight recovery.

## Current proposal and shared-rate refinements

Random command candidates were superseded after pilot root-concentration failures. The current guide partitions the uniform turn and Mach priors into equal-mass cells and uses every discrete altitude level. Cell-centre forecasts choose a cell with probability q; the actual command is drawn uniformly inside that cell (or at its discrete altitude). The exact command-density correction is 1/(K*q). Thus numerical cell centres do not discretise the physical continuous command prior. A defensive uniform mixture preserves support. A BFO-only contact may additionally guide toward the next BTO; that future evidence enters the physical likelihood only at its own epoch. This is an importance proposal, not a requirement for constant future controls.

The shared rate lambda=1/tau has conditional log density in log(lambda) equal to N*log(lambda)-R*lambda, for event count N and total non-busy waiting exposure R across the three manoeuvre clocks. Conditional rejection sampling refreshes this rate at propagation boundaries. It preserves the original common-rate path law while avoiding a permanently frozen rate per starting root. A Poisson-mixture check verifies both mean event counts and cross-interval covariance; independent fresh prior rates would fail that covariance check. The current tau state is an auxiliary conditional rate draw, not an immutable per-root parameter.

Initial direction now explicitly means radar ground track for MH370 and the inference-visible true heading for MH371. The sampled weather/wind is used to convert that quantity into each navigation mode's control variable. A five-mode wind invariant verifies that the same observed initial direction describes the same ground velocity, instead of assigning different aircraft directions to heading and track modes. This is based on initial inference inputs, not held-back later positions.

Deterministic observation-specific offspring counts allocate fewer branches to the closely spaced early contacts. Every child's weight is divided by its actual offspring count. Exact finite-state enumeration checks a schedule that includes single-child steps as well as wide branching.

## Rare first turns and sequential comparison

When the no-new-turn forecast strongly disagrees with the forthcoming BFO, a computational proposal oversamples the event that the first turn starts before that contact. If p=1-exp(-duration/tau) is its prior probability and a is the configured boost, its proposal probability is q=p+(1-p)*a. Event times within the interval retain the exact truncated exponential prior density. Event/no-event corrections are p/q and (1-p)/(1-q), respectively. The no-event alternative remains available. Later turns keep their ordinary clock law. An independent exponential survival/timing-integral check verifies both branches. This does not impose a mandatory turn or add a flight hypothesis.

Branching remained unstable in the 10,000-root test. A controlled comparison therefore uses the existing stratified sequential bootstrap engine with the same scientific model and the same corrected transition proposals. It resamples at an ESS fraction of 0.5 while preserving declared initial navigation strata and their evidence weights. Computational branching likelihood-unit multipliers are unused by sequential filtering, whose evidence is computed in physical likelihood units. The initial root count is not a count of independent posterior histories after resampling. Root concentration is only an ancestry diagnostic for this algorithm; independent seeds, increased populations, recovery controls and retained-mode checks decide adequacy. A wrapper enforces the run's wall-clock bound without changing scientific transitions. Scientific source bytes and dependency lockfile are hashed at build time, separately from the executable/sampler identity, to identify controlled comparisons.

Observation blocks: a BFO-only contact and following BTO contact are propagated and scored sequentially at their original times, with all p/q factors retained, before resampling. This is computational scheduling, not a different likelihood or an extra use of the data. Generic singleton identity and exact finite-state controls accompany the adapter.

Integration correction: deterministic drift uses midpoint controls, environment and local-frame transport. Midpoint turn rate uses midpoint Mach/temperature. OU endpoints still have exact finite-time Gaussian laws; their displacement uses trapezoidal quadrature, checked against the independently integrated stationary OU covariance. Cruise step 20s and manoeuvre step 1s are numerical settings. Eastward ellipsoidal-parallel reference and combined-turn/Mach/climb refinement support the corrected deterministic integrator. Further density-level step refinement remains required.

Conditional turn-time proposal: when the existing predictive-BFO innovation triggers oversampling of a first turn at a BFO-only contact, divide the exponential time law conditional on an event into 12 equal-probability cells. Score cell centers using 128 prior-angle cells and the existing current-BFO/future-BTO guide. Select a time cell with a 20% defensive uniform mixture, then sample continuously inside its probability interval. Retain exact 1/(K*q_cell), the separate event/no-event correction, and the subsequent command proposal correction. Coast forecasts, cell centers and guide widths affect proposals only. Discarded initial conditional-time draws add no information or weight. The exponential antiderivative control verifies interval mass and time moment even under an intentionally wrong guide.

Corrected next-contact potential: sequential resampling occurs at every actual contact as required by ESS. At a BFO-only contact, forecast the next contact having BTO, using the current nominal command and the existing broadened BTO/BFO guide. Form a peak-relative positive potential h=0.001+0.999*exp(log_guide_shape), capped at one. The incremental physical weight is multiplied by h_new/h_old; h is forced to one at the final observation. Hence every artificial potential cancels in the complete path weight. The next-contact BTO and BFO are still actually assimilated only at their own timestamps. This is computational guidance and does not widen physical observation noise. The generic exact finite-state posterior/evidence control passes with an intentionally biased guide; a zero-guide Gaussian run is bit-identical to the unwrapped filter. No contact blocks are used with this method.
