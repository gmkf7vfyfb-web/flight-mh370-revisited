How selecting simpler courses changes the 00:11 distribution

This compares the full 1,020,000-candidate source calculation with explicit selections for at most two turns, at most one turn, and constant true track with at most one turn. The source's speed and altitude adjustments remain available. These selections express conditional hypotheses about behaviour; they add no observed evidence.

The percentage retained is the fraction of the broad model's computed probability that meets each selection. Each selected distribution is then normalized to one. A smaller area describes uncertainty conditional on that selection; it does not reduce the uncertainty of the broad case.

  • Selections are conditional hypotheses, not observed facts or a calibrated intent/coherence model.
  • Turn counts are command starts after the configured limit epoch. Commands before that epoch remain unrestricted by this selection.

  • Conditioning cannot recover broad-model trajectories missed by the sampler. Initial candidates and their descendants are not independent posterior draws.
