# Results and decision

Updated 08 September 2026, 14:36 UTC

The useful achievement is a repaired and checked flight-to-impact calculation, 215 explicit flight examples, and a clearer account of what the evidence can support. The examples reach about 15–37°S at 00:11 under the declared flight and fuel models. They do not measure the full possible region or its probability. Broad probability sampling failed independent-run checks, and none of the 44,597 tested final-BFO pairs satisfies both readings under the audited conditions. The defensible material is the conditional sensitivity and validation evidence; a combined crash-location PDF and an optimal search box have not been established.

Finding a few routes that fit is much easier than assigning reliable probabilities to all routes that could fit. The broader samplers repeatedly concentrate on different small parts of that space, so a smooth-looking plot can conceal a failed calculation. Missing aircraft, startup and evidence calibration adds a separate problem that more particles cannot solve. Future work needs a small, explicit scientific question, independent known-answer checks before scaling, one maintained implementation, and a firm stop when extra work does not stabilize the result.

The active task is a reliable broad trajectory distribution through 00:11. Pete has authorized trying forward/backward updates, beginning with four direction changes and two Mach and two altitude changes, then ensembles in the thousands and progressively larger allowances if stable. End-of-flight and final BFOs are outside this trial. The alternative paper is not accepted. A separate bounded investigation records all new numerical work.
