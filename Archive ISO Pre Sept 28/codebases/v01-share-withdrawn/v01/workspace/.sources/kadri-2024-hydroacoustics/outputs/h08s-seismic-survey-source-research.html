MH370 hydroacoustic source audit · 3 September 2026

The repeating Diego Garcia signal has an identified survey source

Public records place two large seismic surveys off north-west Australia on 8 March 2014. ATSB went further: it obtained both operators’ navigation data and matched the H08S bearing evolution and last shot to TGS Huzzas.

Bottom line

The approximately ten-second H08S train should be treated as identified Huzzas airgun clutter. Public AIS would be a useful independent visual reproduction, but it is no longer needed to establish the source. The higher-value data are the shot-by-shot TGS navigation used by ATSB and the calibrated H08S triad waveforms.

is the extrapolated pressure amplitude; Kadri’s stated airgun range is 2–4 Pa.

This is an order-of-magnitude consistency check, not a fitted propagation model or an energy inversion.

It omits bathymetry, directionality, frequency-dependent loss, sound-speed structure, Deep Sound Channel coupling, station response and filtering. ATSB’s observation that Huzzas was detected but a similarly powerful Centaurus source was not shows why path physics matters.

Historical AIS: what is actually online

No openly downloadable Geo Caspian track for 8 March 2014 surfaced in this search.

  • Global Fishing Watch’s timebar reaches back to 2012 and can search vessel tracks by identity; API access requires a token. Coverage of this non-fishing vessel remains unverified.
  • Commercial AIS archives may hold 2014 satellite messages, but coverage and retention for this exact area/date must be confirmed before purchase.
  • Search using the stable identifier IMO 9525560; names, flag and MMSI can change.

Even a genuine AIS track would describe the vessel, not necessarily each fired source position. Survey-navigation data are the stronger record.

Energy: a defensible coarse comparator

A measured/modelled 2,600 in³ north-west Australian array—not Huzzas itself—has a directional per-shot sound-exposure source level of 228 dB re 1 μPa²·m²·s. An isotropic conversion gives roughly 0.52 MJ of acoustic energy and, under spherical spreading alone, roughly 1.8 nJ m⁻² at 4,763 km.

Do not compare that fluence directly with Kadri’s 2–4 Pa peak. Energy requires the calibrated pressure waveform integrated over time: E/A = ∫p(t)²dt/(ρc). Peak Pa, RMS Pa and SEL are different observables.

The comparator is useful only for establishing scale. The correct next step is to combine Huzzas shot navigation, raw calibrated H08S waveforms, bathymetry and a range-dependent propagation model.
