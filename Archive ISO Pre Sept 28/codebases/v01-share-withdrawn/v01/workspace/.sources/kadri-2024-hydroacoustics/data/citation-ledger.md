## Citation decisions

- `FOUND`: the official-review outcome; the p. 9 prose inconsistency; the distinct approximately 00:52/57° rectangle 1 and 00:54:30/306.18° preferred rectangle 2 signals; the two selected Table 1 rows; airgun label; prior filtering; and cross-channel bearing method.
- `DERIVED`: the 9.9-second periodicity, filter residuals and spatial intersections.
- `UNKNOWN`: whether any residual is a physical non-airgun event, whether either
  displayed feature is common between stations, and any MH370 association.
