# ATSB 2015 end-of-flight systems evidence

Source: Australian Transport Safety Bureau, *MH370 - Definition of Underwater
Search Areas*, 3 December 2015, Transport Safety Report AE-2014-054.

Official PDF:
https://www.atsb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-12/AE-2014-054_MH370-Definition%20of%20Underwater%20Search%20Areas_3Dec2015.pdf

Version inspected: the official 28-page PDF exposed by ATSB on 25 August 2026,
identified on its cover as 3 December 2015 and AE-2014-054. The full text was
retrieved through the official URL; the PDF is not copied into this repository.

The relevant claims and located passages are recorded in
`citation-ledger.md`. The report supports a conditional systems sequence of
about 60 seconds from dual-bus loss to APU generator availability followed by
about 60 seconds from SDU power restoration to log-on request. It also makes
usable APU fuel conditional on pitch and acceleration.

Result: this evidence supports a named nominal flameout/APU/log-on hypothesis,
not a direct flameout observation and not a calibrated lag-time distribution.
The public report gives approximate component times but no raw manufacturer
test results, run-to-run scatter, or probability of fuel unporting.

Not integrated: no flameout-time likelihood has yet been added to the canonical
runner. The evidence first needs an explicit structural-alternative model so
that the 00:19 log-on is not counted both as a timing observation and as proof
of fuel exhaustion.
