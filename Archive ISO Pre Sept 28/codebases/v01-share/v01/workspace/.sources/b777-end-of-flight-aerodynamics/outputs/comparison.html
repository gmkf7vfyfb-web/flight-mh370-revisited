No located public, legally redistributable candidate provides B777-200ER/Trent 892B lift, drag, moments, control effectiveness, running and windmilling-engine forces, and control-mode behavior across cruise through post-flameout transonic/high-AoA flight with traceable aircraft-specific validation.

The audit publishes no implementation-ready aerodynamic parameters.

What would unblock admission

Obtain a distributable B777-200ER/Trent 892B aerodynamic, propulsion, actuator, and flight-control dataset with stated axes, units, configurations, uncertainty, and validation over the required envelope; or obtain permission to publish a reduced model and independent fixtures derived from such a dataset.
